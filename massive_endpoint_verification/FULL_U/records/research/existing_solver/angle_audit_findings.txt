Independent fixed-witness angle audit, 2026-10-06

Entry point: research/angle_audit.py
No multipliers were optimized or repaired in this audit.

The auditor rebuilds physical U and normalized V/p for every stored row,
including arbitrary direction normals, tapered strip rows, and crossing
rows X4 and beyond. It uses velocity panels split at each Roy endpoint and
each strip boundary (12,24,48 here), plus endpoint-clustering sine-squared
maps. The first threshold panel is linear in velocity.

Default integration: 80 nodes/panel, spins 0..24, then spins 26..40 with
only the first panel reduced to 20 nodes to avoid overflow of raw P_l.
The resulting support includes every spin through 40; remaining spins are
unproved. External Roy/strip quadrature orders are 64/56, higher than the
training bank defaults.

Two zero-weight probe groups are kept distinct:
  spins 2..20 at s-4 = 1e-4,1e-6,1e-8,1e-10,1e-12;
  spins 2..40 at s-4 = 1e-2,1e-4,1e-6.
The final offset is compared with zero using exactly zero sign tolerance.
The second group is explicitly a finite-offset test, not a proof of the
threshold limit. Positive V/p samples where p>1e4 are separately flagged,
because they expose failures of the optimization's large-p sign guard.

Files: output/angle_audits/summary.csv and one JSON per witness. Duplicate
angles from the positive and negative continuation branches are preserved.

36 witnesses were audited. All pass the low-spin s-4=1e-12 probe, but only
15 pass the dense p>1e4 guard through spin 40. The usable numerical region
is theta=0..80 degrees and 270..345 degrees (including a duplicate theta0).
The guard failures occur at every supplied angle from 82 through255.

The huge replays are not physical improvements: they expose missed
positive-V regions at finite energy and, in some cases, at threshold.
Every result has all_spin_certified=false and continuum_certified=false.
Even a smooth, stable finite-spin replay is not a published-bound claim.
