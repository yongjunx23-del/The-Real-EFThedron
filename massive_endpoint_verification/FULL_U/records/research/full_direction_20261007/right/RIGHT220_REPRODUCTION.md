## Objective, variables, and physical constraints

The normal is (a,b)=(cos(theta),sin(theta)); cardinal 90 degrees is explicitly
stored as (0,1), not the small nonzero cosine returned by floating trigonometry.
The objective is the disk-support dual for a*g0+b*g2 in the existing project
normalization. Real multipliers for the homogeneous dispersive/crossing rows
are unrestricted in sign. For spectral spin l and energy mu, form

    U = a*U0 + sum_i lambda_i*U_i
    V/p = a*(V0/p) + b + sum_i lambda_i*(V_i/p).

The quadrature objective is the sum of positive weights times

    hypot(U,V) + V.

For V<0 the implementation evaluates the algebraically equal stable expression
U*U/(hypot(U,V)-V). It first sums normalized V/p and only then multiplies by p;
this avoids cancelling huge raw p values near threshold.
The objective implementation is DiskSupport in
research/directional_certification/numerical_dual_newton.py, lines 20 onward.

This uses the same relaxed pointwise unitarity disk support as the existing
dual framework. The functional adds forward harmonic/resummed dispersion,
crossing-null, Roy, finite-transfer, and differentiated identities. No
primal feasible scattering amplitude is constructed in this calculation.

The exact 220-row inventory, independent of saved ordering, is:

## Newton/QP algorithm and stopping

The shared solver computes analytic gradient and Hessian of the finite disk
support, equilibrates the Hessian diagonally, whitens its eigensystem, clips
small eigenvalues at eigen_floor, and solves a constrained quadratic model.
CLARABEL uses max_iter250, max_threads2, absolute and relative gap tolerances
1e-10, feasibility tolerance1e-11. The trust radius in whitened coordinates
starts at10. A complete-constraint feasible line search and objective decrease
check is retained; failed steps cannot overwrite an accepted checkpoint.

The new wrapper initially selects the800 most active normalized inequalities,
solves that QP, checks every constraint, adds up to800 violated rows, and
repeats for at most12 rounds before falling back to the full QP. It subtracts
1e-9 times each QP row norm as an inward cushion. A reduced-QP candidate is
accepted only when the maximum normalized residual over the full bank is
<=5e-10. The persistent variants retain the successful active row set between
Newton iterations. The full feasible line search remains independent of that
subset selection.

The starting feasibility repair is a cutting-plane L1 coefficient projection:
HiGHS, primal/dual feasibility tolerances1e-9, time limit45 seconds per LP;
1000 initially selected inequalities and up to1000 added per separation
round, at most20 rounds. The complete projected-bank residual is checked.
Projection is a search step, never evidence of a continuum certificate.

Selected90: initial20 steps at eigen_floor1e-7 on n192, followed by40 steps at
1e-8. Both stopped at requested iteration limits. Selected105:40 steps at
1e-7 on the original n48 cache, also iteration limit. No claim of global
finite-grid optimality is made. The exact witness may be valid even when
its optimizer has not converged, but omitted domains still need proof.
