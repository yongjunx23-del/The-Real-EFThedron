1. Direct comparison target
Joan Elias Miro, Andrea Guerrieri, Mehmet Asim Gumus,
The Phases of the Scalar S-Matrix Island, arXiv:2605.06613v1,
7 May 2026. Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06613v1
Source tar: https://arxiv.org/src/2605.06613v1

Eq. (1) uses T(s,t)=32*pi*sum c_nm sigma^n tau^m, with
sigma=sbar^2+tbar^2+ubar^2, tau=sbar*tbar*ubar, sbar=s-4/3, m^2=1.
Consequently the manuscript massive convention is exactly
g0^m=c_00, g2^m=c_10. No 2, 4, pi or mass conversion is required.
The manuscript's massless convention and the sibling file 'g0 g2.txt'
are different and must not be imported into this massive comparison.

Fig.1 has the outer dual region in red and primal region in green.
The arXiv source includes the vector figure img/fig1_final-crop.pdf.
The source does NOT include optimizer arrays or solver code: its sentence
claiming ancillary code/data exists is commented out (TeX line 783).
The vector polylines have been exported reproducibly by
benchmarks_extract_vectors.py into benchmarks_{dual,primal}_{upper,lower}.csv.

Graphical estimates (not original numerical optimizer values):
dual left/top cusp:     g0 approximately -8.015, g2 approximately 1.082
primal left/top cusp:   g0 approximately -7.056, g2 approximately 0.9297
primal right cusp:      g0 approximately 2.6614, g2 approximately 0.06175
isolated dual max-g0 marker: g0 approximately 2.7278, g2 approximately 0.06
The isolated dual max-g0 marker is not joined to the outer polylines;
the drawn red upper and lower curves stop around g0=2.656. Do not use
the polyline largest x coordinate as the published maximum-g0 bound.

The PDF x-axis minor tick strokes are offset from numeric label centers.
Calibration uses numeric labels, checked against the origin marker. The
y-axis uses exact right-side ticks. Use only ~3 significant figures for
physical comparisons. Many CSV decimals are vector geometry precision.

Manuscript Eq.(26)/A5.35 already reports:
g0 >= -7.81702; g0 <= 2.72322; 0 <= g2 <= 1.05800.
Its improvement over the 2026 graphical outer cusp is independently
confirmed: 20.6059% closure of the lower-g0 primal/dual gap and 15.5818%

This is the manuscript's existing improvement, not a new result of our run.

Assumptions/domain in 2026 source:
- fixed-t twice-subtracted dispersion relation, -28<t<4 (Eq.19).
- partial-wave unitarity disk and crossing, with derivative crossing
  equations about s=t=u=4/3 (Eq.23; first nonzero (n,m)=(1,3)).
- dual Roy equations external window 4<=s<=12: the paper explicitly
  attributes this to both partial-wave convergence and dual feasibility.
- spectral integration continues to infinity; it is not cut at 12.
- primal maximal/Landau analyticity is stronger than dual axiomatic
  analyticity (main text). Primal numerical region is not a substitute
  for a rigorous universal outer bound.
- The paper has evident normalization typos away from Eq.1: e.g. the
  text around Eq.20 omits 32*pi when describing c00 and Eq.24 appears
  to omit the nonforward Legendre factor in the c10 sum rule. Preserve
  the internally consistent normalization from Eq.1 and manuscript Eq.23.

2. Older coordinate benchmark
Andrea Guerrieri and Amit Sever, Rigorous bounds on the Analytic S-matrix,
arXiv:2106.10257v2, PRL 127, 251601 (2021).
https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.10257
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.10257
Uses g0=T(4/3,4/3)/(32*pi), hence same massive g0.
Appendix G explicitly quotes g0<=2.7272 and g0>=-8.08.
Important numerical qualification: the lower result is extrapolated in
Jmax, with a difference 0.004 from Jmax=26 and remaining intermediate-
energy outer-region dual violations discussed in the text. Treat as the
published numerical benchmark, not an interval-certified result.
Maximum-g0 figure also quotes Nw extrapolations. The final manuscript
maximum 2.72322 already slightly improves this 2.7272 comparison.

3. Stronger-assumption dual comparison to avoid conflating
Yifei He and Martin Kruczenski, arXiv:2103.11484v2.
https://arxiv.org/html/2103.11484v2
Regularized Mandelstam-representation primal and corresponding dual.
Main text Fig.1 gives pi*F(4/3,4/3,4/3)/4 approximately 2.6613 as a
primal value including a threshold pole. Their dual bounds are strict
for the regularized primal problem, not automatically the same minimal
fixed-t analyticity space. Do not claim that beating 2.7272 beats every
published dual bound without specifying assumptions.

4. Potential source of genuinely new constraints
Miro, Guerrieri, Gumus, Zahed, A Geometric View on Crossing-Symmetric
Dispersion Relations, arXiv:2509.14170v1.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14170
https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14170v1
The abstract states an all-energy extension of Roy-like equations based
on rigorous analyticity. 2605.06613 App.A.2 explicitly references it as
the alternative to their fixed-t window s<=12. Derive and check the
actual crossing-symmetric kernels/domain before using them; extending
the ordinary fixed-t projection by fiat is not equivalent.
