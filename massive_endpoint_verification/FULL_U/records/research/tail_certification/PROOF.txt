ALL-SPIN SIGN CERTIFICATE FOR THE FIXED RESUMMED UPPER-g0 WITNESS
================================================================

This note concerns the V/p sign and coercivity of this FIXED witness. It does
not certify its finite-spin support integral, and is not a certificate for
other optimized witnesses. In particular, enriched Roy or OddRoy witnesses
must be independently checked. Binary64 multipliers are interpreted as exact

row labels are interpreted as exact decimals; strip T is exactly 4/3.

Conventions
-----------
c=4/3, delta=mu-4, Q=mu-c, Z=Q/delta,
p_l=P_l(Z)/Q^3, T_l=l(l+1), tau=T_l/delta.
The physical partial-wave disk is r^2+(b-1)^2<=1. The dual pointwise support is
sqrt(U^2+V^2)+V. Negative V has the stable equivalent U^2/(sqrt(U^2+V^2)-V).
The common positive integration measure is dnu_l=(2l+1)dmu/(2*pi*rho(mu)).

After adding all homogeneous rows, alpha=-1.4492417742900348... is the total
effective coefficient of the forward g0 representation. The coefficient of
X4 is lambda_X=-23.90335355242552...<0; write eta=-lambda_X.

The combined source density W includes BOTH source Jacobians:
- Roy x in[0,1], z in[0,1]: 2 sum lambda_Jn P_n(2x-1)P_J(z),
  s=4+(79/10)x, t=-(79/20)x(1-z).
- Strip x,y in[0,1]: sum lambda_jn[1-(2x-1)^2]P_n(2x-1)P_j(2y-1),

Kernel splitting
----------------
For external s,t,u=4-s-t, define
K1=1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)-1/Q-1/(mu-2c+t),
K2=1/(mu-t)-2/Q+1/(mu-2c+t).
Then L_l/P_l(Z)=K1 P_l(y)/P_l(Z)+K2, y=1+2t/delta.
The apparent pole mu-2c+t is removable in the combined expression.
Its scalar version is L_0=1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-t)+1/(mu-u)-3/Q.

For Roy l>Jmax=6, angular orthogonality removes the PV residue exactly. The
regular kernel is
Lreg/Pstar = [(1-z)/delta] D_l(y,z)/Pstar
 + [P_l(y)/Pstar]*(1/(mu-u)-1/Q-1/(mu-2c+t)) + K2,
where D_l(a,b)=(P_l(a)-P_l(b))/(a-b), continuously extended to a=b.
Equivalently Lreg=L_l-P_l(z)/(mu-s).

Two Legendre inequalities used throughout
-----------------------------------------
For Z>1 and l>=4,
 X4/p = [2Z^2 P_l''(Z)-3ZP_l'(Z)]/[Q^2 P_l(Z)]
       >= l(2l-5)/Q^2 >0.
Proof: roots of P_l and P_l' are real in(-1,1) and symmetric. Pairing +/-roots
shows ZP_l'/P_l>=l and ZP_l''/P_l'>=l-1. Substitute these in the numerator.

For even l and 1<=Y<=Z, root pairing also gives
 P_l(Y)/P_l(Z) <= (Y/Z)^l.
The physical interval bounds are |P_l(x)|<=1 and |P_l'(x)|<=T_l/2.
For Y>=1, P_l'(Y)/P_l(Y)<=T_l/2 and <=l/sqrt(Y^2-1).
The first follows by monotonicity of the logarithmic derivative; the second
follows by differentiating the positive Laplace integral for P_l.
For |a|,|b|<=Y, |D_l(a,b)|<=P_l'(Y).

P_{n+1}(Z)/P_n(Z)>=Z follows inductively from the three-term recurrence.
Thus T_n/P_n(Z) decreases with n>=L whenever Z>=(L+2)/L. This condition holds
on mu<=49 for L>=42 because Z>=143/135>44/42. It justifies replacing every
T_l/P_l(Z) by T_L/P_L(Z) in the compact physical-angle estimates.

UV domain: mu>=49 and all even l>=L
-----------------------------------
Let K=Q^3 K1, H=Q^3 K2, and
 K_inf=2(s-c)(c-u)>=0, H_inf=2(t-c)^2>=0.
The complete normalized kernel is A(mu)+N_l(mu)/Pstar+lambda_X X4/p.

Exact integration of the positive-source correction
---------------------------------------------------
The first verifier used cellwise upper t endpoints. The stronger v2 verifier
instead forms the nonnegative Bernstein majorant Fplus of (W K_inf)_+ on
each source cell. It computes both C0=integral Fplus and
C1=integral Fplus*(-t) exactly. Then
 integral (W K_inf)_+ min(2,tau|t|) <= min(2C0,tau C1)
on each cell. The product moment uses the exact expectation of the affine
coordinates against each Bernstein basis function; for Roy t it factors as
x*(1-z). This removes avoidable endpoint overestimation. Both versions are
valid; v2 has separate cache names.
