3. CONVENTIONS AND GRID DETAILS

m=1; rho(mu)=sqrt(1-4/mu); only even spins.
dnu_l=(2l+1)dmu/(2*pi*rho).
f_l=r_l+i*b_l and the local disk is r_l^2+(b_l-1)^2<=1.
An affine dual witness has objective integral[U_l*r_l+V_l*b_l]dnu_l.
The unconstrained disk support is sqrt(U^2+V^2)+V.
For V<0 it is evaluated as U^2/(sqrt(U^2+V^2)-V), avoiding cancellation.
p_l=P_l(Z)/Q^3>0, Q=mu-4/3, Z=Q/(mu-4).
V/p is assembled before multiplying by p to avoid subtracting separately
huge threshold kernels. Source research/dual_newton.py:15 and
research/angle_audit.py:68,:139,:146.

research/existing_solver/massive_forward_kernels.py:1; main search copies

4. OPTIMIZATION, PRECONDITIONING, AND GUARDS

Main engine research/dual_newton.py:77 minimizes the finite-grid disk
support using constrained damped Newton steps. It diagonal-equilibrates
the Hessian, eigendecomposes its symmetric form, floors eigenvalues at
max(maxeig*1e-8,1e-12), and whitens correlated row directions.
The step QP uses CLARABEL through CVXPY, max_iter250, gap abs/rel1e-10,
feasibility1e-11. Constraint rows are scaled by Euclidean norm.
Default whitened trust radius10, expands to at most100 or contracts to.05.
An initial feasibility projection uses row scales from weighted U and
normalized V. Line search allows70 halvings, Armijo factor1e-4, and a.995
interior fraction for approaching constraints. Failed QP/line-search
steps do not replace a better accepted incumbent.
These transformations condition the finite optimization; they do not
certify physical amplitudes or improve mathematical completeness.

Right angle_search.py:13 uses eps2e-6, ftol3e-11, gtol1e-6, with p>1e4
sign constraints. Left angle_constraints_experiment.py:91,:111,:129 uses

HiGHS, row and column scaling, and then eps1e-8 in the Newton engine.
The exact final lower witness is a further algebraic repair, not an

6. APPROXIMATIONS, FAILURES, AND CONTRADICTIONS TO AVOID

All new nontrivial inequalities are exploratory finite-spin numerical
candidates. Remaining work: all-energy continuum errors, endpoints, all
omitted energies for ell42..100, every spin>100, and joint large-spin/energy
limits. Finite negative samples, a finite maximum probed spin1000, and
necessary leading-crossing signs do NOT solve these problems.
No solver iteration limit or failed Newton QP is global optimality.
No stored phase-derived g0_phase/g2_phase is a feasible primal amplitude:
at approximate optimization, null moments need not vanish exactly. Use
the disk support objective and its audited multipliers as the candidate.

Right A-tail calculation: output/angle_audits_resummed/tail_A_envelope.json,
research/existing_solver/tail_envelope.py:21 and tail_envelope_derivation.md.
Analytic inverse-Legendre sum bound is used, but its final scalar and angular
integrals are ordinary floating quadrature, NOT directed intervals.
With assumed independently valid global G=1.09: L40 A=1.3239477586782667e-5,
sqrt(2GA)=.005372342239581002. For L100 A=2.583128708959778e-8,
sqrt(2GA)=.00023730192973366897. A full tail bound additionally requires
V/p<=eta everywhere, giving sqrt(2GA)+G*max(eta,0). Such a global V bound
was NOT proved. In particular do not report head+.00537234 as a certified
bound. The A-tail script is valid only for its supported forward/strip
family; it does not handle the final left derivative rows.
