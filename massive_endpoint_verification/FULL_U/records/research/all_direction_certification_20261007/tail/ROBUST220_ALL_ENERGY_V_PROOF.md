## Normalization and exact source splitting

Let c=4/3,delta=mu-4,Q=mu-c,Z=Q/delta,T=ell(ell+1),p=P_ell(Z)/Q^3.
The Roy source variables are s in[4,119/10],z in[0,1],
t=-(s-4)(1-z)/2,u=4-s-t. The folded weight W includes the factor2.
The strip sources have s in[12,24] or[24,48], t in[-c,0], with tapered
polynomial energy weights. All source measures are exactly those in the
existing null construction and moment builders.

With y=1+2t/delta, write

```
L/Pstar = K1 R + K2,
R=P_ell(y)/P_ell(Z),
K1=1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)-1/Q-1/(mu-2c+t),
K2=1/(mu-t)-2/Q+1/(mu-2c+t),
Kscalar=K1+K2=1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-t)+1/(mu-u)-3/Q.
```

For ell>6, Roy angular orthogonality permits subtracting
`P_ell(z)/(Pstar*(mu-s))` before source integration. Denote the resulting
regular normalized kernel by Lreg. This subtraction integrates to zero at
each source s, including its PV interpretation.

## A uniform compact Roy bound requiring only the existing source sign

Split the Roy source into E={t<-(c+delta)} and its complement. On E,
y<-Z and evenness gives R>=1. The physical relation between s,t,z implies

```
s-mu >= 2c+delta.
```

The factorization

```
K1=(s-c)(c-u)(2mu+t-4)/[(mu-s)(mu-u)Q(mu-2c+t)]
```

shows K1>=0 on E: the two potentially negative denominator factors are both
negative, and the numerator is positive on the complete source domain.
The existing exact Roy certificate proves W>=0 for t<=-c, hence on E.
Consequently the contribution of `W K1(R-1)` to minus the source integral
is nonpositive and may be discarded. The remainder is bounded below by

```
Lreg >= -(1+1/Pstar)/(2c+delta)-3/Q.
```

On E complement, both y and z lie in[-Z,Z]. The divided difference bound
`|D_ell(a,b)|<=P_ell'(Z)`, together with
`(Z-1)P_ell'(Z)<=ell P_ell(Z)`, gives

```
|Lreg| <= 3ell/(2c)+2/mu+3/Q.
```

To see the factors, use the nonsingular expression

```
Lreg = (1-z)/delta *D_ell(y,z)/Pstar
      + R[1/(mu-u)-1/Q]+1/(mu-t)-2/Q
      -2/delta*D_ell(y,-Z)/Pstar.
```

Here mu-u>=mu and mu-t>=mu. For ell>=1 this latter bound also dominates
the discarded-region remainder. Therefore the entire negative Roy source
contribution is at most

```
Q^3 * integral|W| * [3ell/(2c)+2/mu+3/Q].
```

This is polynomial in spin and contains no uncontrolled exponential angular
factor. It does not require the stronger t<=-3/5 source sign used by the old150
proof; that stronger sign is false for this witness.

For strips on4<mu<=8 there is no source PV pole. The same divided-difference
bound gives

```
|L/Pstar| <= ell/c+1/(12-mu)+2/mu+3/Q.
```

Thus on4<mu<=8 the source bound is linear in ell. The proof uses exact
Bernstein upper norms of the combined signed densities, generated by
`compact_source_data.compute`; its ignored forward/derivative rows do not
enter these source norms.

## Physical-angle source bound on8<=mu<=49

Every source y lies in[-1,1], because delta>=4 and -t<=79/20. Hence |P_ell(y)|<=1.
The Roy PV divided difference uses the physical derivative bound T/2 and is
bounded by `T/(2delta)`. After division byPstar, the remaining K1 terms are
bounded by

```
Roy: Rabs [T/(2delta)+1/mu+1/Q+1/(mu-2c-79/20)],
strip: sum_(A,B)2D_AB/(B-A)+Sabs[1/mu+1/Q+1/delta].
```

Here `D_AB=sum |lambda_jn|[4+n(n+1)]` bounds the derivative of the tapered
energy polynomial in its normalized coordinate. The standard subtraction
formula for its Hilbert transform bounds the integral by2D_AB/(B-A), including
both endpoints where the taper vanishes. This is the same already-reviewed
strip-PV bound used in the original compact certificate.

The normalized K2 term is separated exactly:

```
Q^3 K2 = 2(t-c)^2/[1-((t-c)/Q)^2].
```

Its absolute integral is bounded by `norm2/[1-(317/(60 Qmin))^2]`,
where Qmin=20/3 and norm2 is the exact combined-source Bernstein norm of
`W*2(t-c)^2`.

## Fifth-power crossing coercivity

The full X4..X8 crossing contribution divided byp is the expectation of
the exact polynomial F(k,1/Q) under the positive Legendre Taylor weights.
An exact Bernstein expansion in z=1/Qin[0,3/8] proves

```
F(k,z) <= M-a*(k-10)_+^5,
a=684948565004237343/46116860184273879040 >0,
M=57406947235605656793/5764607523034234880.
```

All coefficients in k-10 of degree0..6 are nonpositive; the fifth coefficient
is at most-a. For k0..9, M is an exact Bernstein upper bound. Jensen gives

```
crossing/p <= M-a*(m-10)_+^5,
m=(Z-1)P_ell'(Z)/P_ell(Z).
```

The function `(k-10)_+^5` is convex, so the positive-part Jensen step is valid
even when some of the Legendre mass lies below10.

At fixed Z, the positive moment representation forP_ell proves log-convexity
in ell. The Legendre derivative recurrence then gives

```
m_ell/ell=[Z-P_(ell-1)(Z)/P_ell(Z)]/(Z+1) >= m_L/L, ell>=L.
```

Also m_L increases withZ because its derivative with respect tolog(Z-1) is
the variance of k. On4<mu<=8 use the endpointmu8 and the lower ratio
r=m_L(5/3)/L. The source grows only linearly in ell, while the crossing upper
bound is `M-a*(r ell-10)^5`; the code checks its derivative is negative atL.
This closes all larger spins. On8<=mu<=49 the remaining noncrossing terms
are spin-independent upper bounds and m_ell>=m_L(Z(mu49)) suffices.

## Coarse but finite forward/derivative bounds

For complex transfer |t|<=1 set A=2+t/2,q=1+Aw and w=1/delta.
The analytic factors are

```
Q^3 F=8A^2(1+8w/3)^3/q^3,
omega=(Aw+i sqrt(1+(4+t)w))^2/q^2.
```

Since |q|>=1+3w/2 and |A|<=5/2, uniformly w>=0,

```
|Q^3 F| <= 50*(16/9)^3,
|omega| <=100/9.
```

The branch point of the square root lies outside the unit transfer disk.
Cauchy's coefficient estimate bounds each transfer Taylor coefficient by
the corresponding displayed supremum. Apply it to
`Q^3 F*omega^j*(1-omega)^(2p-2)` for derivative order p<=3.
The Legendre transfer coefficient of degree k is at most
`T^k/(delta^k*(k!)^2)`. Thus the total forward numerator overPstar is bounded
by `sum_(k=0)^3 C_k*T^k/(delta^k*Pstar)` with exact rational C_k saved in JSON.
The ordinary physical harmonics use |omega|=1, rather than the complex bound.
The rational harmonic rows are bounded directly on the unit circle using
their denominator lower bound `(1-r)^2` and exact positive factor
`1+2r*cos(phi)+r^2`.

For the regular derivative subtractions, the exact rational Ka,Kb factors
give Cauchy suprema

```
|Q^3 Ka|<=3136/81,
|Q^3 Kb|<=6272/81.
```

These follow from |3t-4|<=7 and comparison of
`(8w+3)^2` with `(64/3)(3w+2)(w+3)` or
`(64/3)(3w+1)(w+3)`. Ka joins the forward coefficient bound; Kb is outsidePstar.
The g0 homogeneous subtraction outsidePstar is bounded by128|alpha|/27.
No harmonic H1 is imposed as an extra null.

The positive Legendre term of degreeK=64 gives

```
P_ell(Z) >= delta^-K *c^K/(K!)^2 *prod_(j<K)(T-j(j+1)).
```

For T>=T_L, each product factor grows at least asT/T_L. Consequently, for
k<=3 anddelta<=D, the ratioT^k/(delta^k Pstar) is bounded by its simple exact
majorant `T_L^k D^(K-k)/A_K(L)`. This controls the low and middle compact
regions without a spin cutoff approximation.

## Finite-UV contraction on0<=tau<=256

`contracted_forward.py` eliminates complex cancellation before interval
evaluation. For real transfer, |omega(t)|=1 and

```
x(t)=Re omega(t)=2A^2 w^2/q^2-1,
Re[omega^j(1-omega)^(2p-2)]
 =sum_h(-1)^h binom(2p-2,h) T_(j+h)(x(t)).
```

All real harmonic and derivative coefficients are contracted exactly over
rational polynomials inw. For rational harmonic rows, the identity
`Re[N(omega)/D(omega)]=Re[N(omega)D(1/omega)]/|D(omega)|^2`
produces exact real Chebyshev rational functions. Their real parts vanish
quadratically atw0, a cancellation lost by direct complex interval arithmetic.
Every resulting polynomial is shifted exactly to the midpoint of its w cell
before Arb Horner evaluation. The small regular subtraction jets retain their
exact finite-energy rational formulas.

The source and crossing enclosures are those of the previously reviewed
finite-UV Region implementation. The objective adds exactly+1 toV/p.
The new full adaptive calculation atL450 accepts only finite directed bounds
strictly below-1/1000. It has788 leaves. Fresh Arb320 and Arb400 runs are saved.
This is a finite-spin enclosure overeta in[0,1/(450*451)], not a limiting
curve or interpolation between sampled spins.

## Closure for unboundedtau>=256 andmu>=49

At fixedtau, the positive Legendre coefficient of degreek is proportional to

```
(c*tau)^k/(k!)^2 *prod_(j<k)(1-j(j+1)/T).
```

IncreasingT increases the ratio of coefficients monotonically in k, and adds
only higher-index terms. Thus monotone likelihood-ratio ordering implies
`m_ell(tau)>=m_L(tau)>=m_L(256)`. Apply the same fifth-power crossing bound.

For |t|<=1,w<=1/45, the sharper elementary bounds
`|omega|<5/4` and `|Q^3 F|<64` replace the coarse compact constants. The positive
Legendre term K20 bounds `tau^k/Pstar` uniformly fortau>=256,k<=3.

No PV pole occurs becausemu>=49 is above every source energy. The exact
finite-to-limit K1 ratio is

```
R1=(1+(2+t/2)w)(1+8w/3)^2 /
   [(1-(s-4)w)(1+(s+t)w)(1+(c+t)w)].
```

Use s-4<=44,s+t>=0,c+t>=-157/60 to obtain the uniform positive ratio bound
in `large_tau_highspin220.py`. The K1 term is suppressed byPstar. The K2
bound usesQ>=143/3. Together these giveV/p<-423, rounded conservatively to<-1.

## Separate sharper compact component: all even ell>=110

The original450 component files remain unchanged. The separate
`compact_highspin220_sharp.py` improves two coarse estimates and proves the
same compact sign `V/p<-1` for every even ell>=110 on4<mu<=49. Thus only
compact spins42..108 require a direct finite-spin bridge. This does not change
the UV cutoff450 established above.

First, the exact logarithmic derivative of the analytic conformal factor is

```
d_t log(omega) = -i*w/[(1+A*w)*sqrt(1+2*A*w)], A=2+t/2.
```

For complex |t|<=1, its modulus is at most
`w/[(1+3w/2)*sqrt(1+3w)] <=1/4`. The latter is an elementary polynomial
inequality, since

```
(1+3w/2)^2(1+3w)-16w^2
 =1+w*(27w^2/4-19w/4+6)>0, w>=0.
```

The quadratic is strictly positive because its discriminant is negative and
its leading coefficient is positive. The conformal factor is analytic and
nonzero on this disk, so an analytic logarithm exists. Integrate from the
real pointt0, where|omega|=1, to obtain `|omega(t)|<=exp(1/4)<4/3`.
The final strict inequality follows from
`log(4/3)=integral_1^(4/3)dx/x>1/4`.

Replace the compact Cauchy constant100/9 by4/3. No physical assumptions or
kernel formulas change.

Second, the positive Legendre monomial degreeK in `ratio_bound` is selected
from4..L to maximize its exact rational value at the worst energy endpointD.
Every candidate K separately proves the same ratio inequality used above;
choosing the strongest one is valid and the selection uses exact Fraction
ordering. Degrees near the dominant term are much stronger than fixedK64
whenL is moderate. The low and middle compact domains may select differentK.
