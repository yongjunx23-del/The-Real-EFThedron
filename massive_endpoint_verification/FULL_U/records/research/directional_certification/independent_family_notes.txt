DIRECTIONAL CERTIFICATION REVIEW, 6 OCTOBER 2026

Family and normalization
========================
Start with the fixed150-row witness proving a bound on g0. For exact slopeb
and exact X4 multiplier incrementd, keep every real field unchanged and set
 V_new=V_old+b*p+d*X4.
This is a valid linear dual functional for g0+b*g2 because X4 is a homogeneous
crossing null. Its support must still be integrated or bounded over EVERY
energy and even spin. Linearity alone does not guarantee the dual integral
is finite or the old omitted-spin negativity remains true.

For beta=sqrt(1-4/mu), x=(1-beta^2)/4=1/mu, c=4/3,
 Q=mu-c, delta=mu-4, Z=Q/delta,
 p/e^2=x*P_l(Z)/[16(1-c*x)^3], wheree=1-beta^2.
For each finite spin, this rational expression and its exact interval
derivatives provide the additive head term. The X4 expression is
 X4/e^2=x^3[2Z^2 P_l''(Z)-3ZP_l'(Z)]/[16(1-c*x)^5].
These agree with research/directional_certification/head_family.py.

The actual certified halfplane is g0+b*g2<=C_b. Its unit-angle support is
C_b/sqrt(1+b^2) at theta=atan(b), with -90<theta<90degrees. Do not multiply
by a negative cosine to claim a opposite-facing halfplane; this family does
not cover the left half of angular directions. Rational b avoids storing
inexact trigonometric coefficients in the certificate.

Exact crossing facts
=====================
 chi_l(mu)=X4/p=[2Z^2P_l''(Z)-3ZP_l'(Z)]/[Q^2P_l(Z)].
chi_0=0. At spin2,
 chi_2=-6/[3Q^2-delta^2],
 lim_(mu->4)chi_2=-9/32, lim_(mu->infinity)chi_2=0 frombelow.
Thus d>=32b/9 is sufficient to avoid increasing the spin2 threshold
constant for positiveb, but this condition alone says nothing about other
energies. At equality,
 b+(32b/9)*chi_2=b*delta*(delta+8)/(delta^2+8delta+32/3),
positive for everydelta>0 and approachingb in the UV.

The implemented head choice d=4b for b>0 gives threshold addition-b/8,
which leaves a useful strictly negative margin. For b<=0,d=0 is safe and
decreases V/p uniformly. The spin4 andspin6 threshold chi values are27/16
and189/32, respectively, so positive d increases their V; their old large
negative margins must still be checked rather than assumed.

For every evenl>=4 andZ>=1,
 chi_l>=l(2l-5)/Q^2>0.
Proof: pair the Legendre roots as+-r_i, put t_i=Z^2/(Z^2-r_i^2)>=1.
ThenQ^2chi=-2sum_i t_i+8sum_(i!=j)t_it_j. Forn=l/2>=2 this polynomial
is increasing in everyt_i, so its minimum is8n(n-1)-2n=l(2l-5).
This lower bound can multiply a NEGATIVE total X4 multiplier in an upper
bound onV. It cannot be multiplied by a positive increment to upper-bound
V_old+d*X4 from the old aggregate bound. The safe positive-d method is to
rerun the old analytic envelope with the total multiplierlambda_X+d<0.
Here lambda_X=-23.90335355242552 as an exact binary64 rational.

Reusable certificates and interpolation
=======================================
U is unchanged, so every old real-norm integral A is reusable exactly.
For negativeb,d=0, an old local coercivitykappa becomeskappa-b; the old
global omitted-spin margin31/50 becomes31/50-b. The source polynomials,
dangerous-source positivity proof, exact moments, and all source norms are
unchanged. The head contribution must nevertheless be recomputed because
the nonlinear disk support depends on the newV.

Given certified endpoint halfplanes atb1,b2, everyb=t*b1+(1-t)*b2,
0<=t<=1, automatically obeys
 g0+b*g2<=t*C_b1+(1-t)*C_b2.
This yields a certified continuous angular sector between successful
slopes. It is a valid interpolation of inequalities, not a claim that each
intermediate value is an independently optimized tangent bound.
