The new Jensen lower bound follows directly from the positive Legendre
Taylor distribution. Put a=c/(mu-4), Z=1+2a, and m=E[k]=a*d_a log P_l(Z).
The exact crossing ratio is
 X4/p=(9/32){E[k(k-1)]-(4/Q)E[k]}
      =(9/32){E[k^2]-(1+4/Q)m}.
Therefore X4/p >=(9/32){m^2-(1+4/Q)m}. The code checks m>(1+4/Q)/2
before increasing the lower bound by monotonicity in m. Negative lambda_X
then turns this lower bound into a safe upper bound for its contribution.

At fixed physical Z>1, m_l/l is nondecreasing in l. Heine's integral gives
 P_l(cosh eta)=average_phi q(phi)^l, q=cosh eta+sinh eta*cos phi>0.
The derivative (1/l)*d_eta logP_l is the q^l-tilted expectation of
q_eta/q. Both q and q_eta/q increase with cos phi, so positive covariance
makes this expectation nondecreasing in l. Multiplication by the positive
factor converting eta-derivatives to a-derivatives gives the claim.
Consequently the compact low-energy use m_l>=l*m_L/L, and the checked
negative derivative of the resulting spin polynomial, cover all l>=L.
The middle-energy weaker assertion m_l>=m_L is also valid.

At fixed joint tau=l(l+1)/(mu-4), Legendre Taylor coefficients are
 a_k(l)=1/(k!)^2 product_{j=0}^{k-1}(1-j(j+1)/(l(l+1))).
For increasing l, the successive coefficient ratios increase, hence the
positive coefficient distribution has increasing mean. This justifies the
finite-L lower mean used in the UV Jensen estimate. Monotonicity in tau
follows from d(log tau)m=Var(k)>=0. The tau>=256 bound uses the largest
allowed coefficient1+4/(49-c), a lower mean at tau256, and negative lambda_X;
thus it closes the unbounded tau interval, rather than sampling a finite cap.

The positive-V support inequality has the correct factor2:
 h(U,V)<=U^2/(2 eta p)+2p*(V/p+eta)_+.
It follows from the2-Lipschitz monotonic dependence of h on V and the disk
bound at V=-eta p. For tau in[a,b], dmu=T/tau^2 dtau,Q>=T/tau gives
 p*dmu<=P*tau/T^2 dtau. The positive Legendre majorant P<=I0(2sqrt(c*b))
and beta>=sqrt(1-4/49), plus
 sum_{integer l>=L}(2l+1)/[l^2(l+1)^2]=1/L^2,
give exactly the stored p-mass bound. Summing all integer spins safely
majorizes the even-spin sum. The code uses the global A40 real norm twice
on disjoint energy pieces, a conservative duplication, not an omission.
