ANGLE 85 / ANGLE 255: UV COERCIVITY COMPONENTS

These are new fixed-witness proofs. Neither inherits a different witness's
numerical interval records. Shared files are previously reviewed algebraic
kernel implementations. All new source contractions and interval records
are bound to each exact NPZ SHA256 and its actual binary64-rational normal.

This component concerns even ell>=42 and mu>=49 only. It does not certify
the finite head, compact-energy coercivity, real-norm integral, or final
support bound by itself. Those are separate same-witness inputs.

NORMALIZATION
delta=mu-4, Q=mu-4/3, c=4/3, Z=Q/delta,
p=P_ell(Z)/Q^3, T=ell(ell+1), tau=T/delta, eta=1/T, w=tau eta.
The spectral field being bounded is V/p in the established dnu convention.
If V/p<=-kappa<0, the physical support contribution is at most
integral U^2/(2 kappa p) dnu. This is why the root combines this component
with separately replayed real-norm bounds on exactly the same domain.

EXACT DIRECTION
The objective is a*g0+b*g2. Source/subtraction construction places a in
the forward alpha. UniformSource.field omits b; the adapter adds exact b.
FiniteUV.field includes the historical unit coefficient +1, so its adapter
adds exact b-1. No rounded degree angle is used to construct a or b.
The campaign modules assert exact equality to the saved NPZ's rational
binary64 entries. The witness bytes and all new source caches are frozen.

SOURCE CONTRACTION
The source uses the original Roy folded normalization (factor two),
B=119/10, and transfer width exactly 4/3 despite decimal text in row names.
On mu>=49 all source Legendre arguments are physical, hence |P_ell|<=1.
The exact rational correction factor is
 R1=[1+(2+t/2)w](1+8w/3)^2 /
    [(1+(4-s)w)(1+(s+t)w)(1+(t+4/3)w)].
Its degree-11 quotient is integrated exactly against the combined source.
The remaining rational numerator has only three coefficients after factoring
w^12. Their absolute integrated bounds, divided by a positive denominator
bound, produce the rigorous remainder. This is an exact division identity,
not an assumption that a geometric series converges. At mu>=49,
the denominator bound (1-44w)(1-157w/60) is strictly positive.

The cache finite_source_N12_K80.pkl contains all 12 by 81 exact rational
source moments and the integrated remainder coefficient bounds. The
zeroth row is checked against the independently prepared joint source
moment array; the finite model also checks the complete first-order row
identity. The signed K2 correction is retained to order 12, with the
remaining positive geometric absolute bound from compact_norms.pkl.

FINITE SPINS 42..448
Every even spin uses its exact finite Legendre coefficient polynomial:
 a_k=prod_{j=0}^{k-1}(1-j(j+1)/T)/(k!)^2.
Pstar=sum_{k=0}^ell a_k(c tau)^k has positive coefficients, so its exact
endpoint values bound the entire positive denominator on a tau interval.
Source k<=min(ell,80) is contracted exactly with the R1 quotient before
interval evaluation. For ell>80, the omitted Legendre source series is
bounded by a Bessel majorant at n=81, using a=79*tau/20 and ratio
a/82^2<1. This is multiplied by norm1*(R1max+R1error); the independent
R1error itself uses |P_ell|<=1, avoiding a false exponential factor.

Crossing is retained exactly for k<=min(ell,63). The omitted k>=64
coefficient polynomial is nonpositive on the entire z=1/Q interval
[0,3/8], as verified by exact rational Bernstein coefficients. Dropping
those terms therefore yields an upper bound. The contracted forward
expression preserves polynomial cancellations before Arb evaluation.

Each spin covers tau in [0,min(256,T/45)], equivalent to mu>=49 up to the
unbounded-tau seam. A first Arb400 adaptive proof requires every interval
finite and its directed upper below -1/10000. It additionally requires
the interval upper no larger than a quarter of the midpoint upper so
that the saved local margins remain useful. Arb480 replays every saved
interval. The final safe local upper is the maximum of the two directed
uppers, never an unchecked nearest floating-point value.

ALL INTEGER SPINS >=450
For tau in [0,256], eta is enclosed on a gapless rational partition of
[0,1/(450*451)]. Angle85 uses 16 panels and kappa=1/1000; angle255 uses
32 panels and kappa=1/4000. Retained source and crossing contractions are
exact bivariate polynomials in tau and eta. The positive Legendre prefix
through k=80 bounds Pstar from below; its endpoint upper plus an explicit
positive Bessel remainder bounds it from above. All retained factors are
nonnegative on this eta interval. The proof applies to physical discrete
eta=1/[ell(ell+1)]; its interval over-cover does not assume a noninteger
Legendre partial wave is physically present. Each tau partition is
replayed at 480 bits after its 400-bit construction.

UNBOUNDED TAU
For ell>=108, mu>=49, tau>=256, positive monomial degree-20 Legendre
bounds suppress forward and first-source terms. The mean of the positive
Legendre coefficient distribution obeys m>=m_108(256)>10 by the reviewed
stochastic ordering in ell and tau.

For angle85 the negative shifted crossing fifth-power term already proves
V/p<-1. For angle255, retain every negative shifted crossing power. If
the exact Bernstein coefficient cap on [0,3/8] is c_j<=0 after shifting
k=y+10, put a_j=-c_j. A separately certified M>=0 bounds k=0,...,9.
Then pointwise F(k,z)<=M-sum_{j>=1}a_j(k-10)_+^j. Convexity gives
 E F <= M-sum_{j>=1}a_j(E k-10)_+^j.
There is no Jensen assumption on the full crossing polynomial and no
j=0 Jensen step. Dropping its nonpositive constant is safe. The new255
bound is below -816 at both 320 and 400 bits, so kappa=1 is conservative.

SEAMS AND ASSEMBLY
For ell<=106, tau<=ell(ell+1)/45<256, so finite tau coverage includes all
mu>=49. For every ell>=108, the unbounded-tau packet closes tau>=256.
For ell>=450 and tau<=256, mu>=4+450*451/256>49; the uniform source
estimate has no hidden compact-energy overlap requirement.

assemble_uv.py checks exact spin lists, exact eta coverage, every exact
tau partition, both precisions, all interval signs, paired references,
crossing omitted-term signs, objective and witness hashes, and recursively
all source manifests. It writes finite_uv_complete.json,
infinite_uv_complete.json, and uv_complete.json. These are UV-component
certificates only. Their scope is explicit; the final physical bound needs
the compact proof, finite-head integral, and same-witness real norms.

REPRODUCTION
Set OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=OMP_NUM_THREADS=MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 in the child
process environment. Avoid concurrent unconstrained numerical-library
imports on this Windows machine; they exhausted virtual commit previously.
For each angle, prepare_angle*.py constructs fresh caches only when absent.
Do not rerun preparation over a frozen completed manifest: use a new output
directory for any change. certify_angle*_finite.py --spins LO:HI constructs
the finite partition; --precision 480 --replay PRODUCER replays it.
Uniform drivers use --group PANEL_LO:PANEL_HI and the analogous replay.
Large-tau drivers run at --precision 320 and 400.
Finally run assemble_uv.py --angle 85 (or 255), after all files are present.
All float summaries and graphical comparisons are diagnostic only.
