# Review of exact positive-mixture head refinement

Reviewed `head/mixture_head.py` against the frozen finite-head interval models and `replay_left_family_fast.py`, including the normalized interval branch and source-error terms.

For positive exact weights x,y, the mixed fields are x(U85,V85)+y(Ug2,Vg2), and the mixed objective/multipliers are the same exact linear combination. The function H(U,V)=sqrt(U^2+V^2)+V is positively homogeneous and subadditive. Therefore convex combinations of both completed witnesses' old costs bound every unrecomputed spin and every source/threshold gap. The two bases have identical exact gap geometry for spins0 and2; that equality is checked, and the union of old ordinary partitions plus gaps covers beta0..1 exactly.

On ordinary cells, direct interval H, the weighted analytic-jet convex endpoint bound, and the negative-V norm estimate use the original reviewed formulas. Positive weights allow source-error bounds to be combined by the triangle inequality. The Legendre factor, Jacobian and normalized geometric p/e factors depend only on spin and interval, so either base provides the common positive geometric factor. The original applicability restriction on the normalized spin0 branch above beta1/2 is preserved. A late audit found that the new mixture wrapper had additionally allowed the Centered spin2 normalized branch outside its Roy real-source activity window; this is not justified by Centered.real_field. Nine selected cells were affected at each precision. A separate frozen repair replaces all nine with fresh actual-field/convex-endpoint enclosures; each correction changes the complete spin2 cost by about7.11e-8. The complete assembler requires the repaired records, verifies unchanged cells and all replacements, and excludes every remaining normalized candidate with beta_upper^2>79/119. Raw mixture head files are diagnostic and are not complete certificates. The original completed base witnesses are unaffected: their Centered trapezoid uses normalized bounds only below beta1/2, while retained higher-spin models use Global/Fast/Quartic source activity. A whole base cell upper bound is also a valid, possibly loose, upper bound for any subinterval, because H is nonnegative. Taking a minimum of valid enclosures remains valid.

Both256-bit and384-bit runs use the same exact partition. Complete assembly retains the larger whole-spin enclosure. The complete assembler rechecks all cell/gap sums and exact coverage, all220 mixed multipliers, exact positive weights and objective, and both original complete certificates' additive decompositions before removing any old spin contribution.

The exact decomposition distinction matters: the85-degree completed certificate uses the maximum of producer and replay per spin, while the g2 completed certificate uses refinedspin0 replay plus all the other original replays. Their producer sums agree at displayed precision but are not identical exact rationals. The new assembler reconstructs the actual retained exact sums, and checks equality with each completed head component before subtraction.

The complete assembler additionally binds the target-direction NPZ and validates all six actual source tables in both moment directories against the already bound moment manifests. This explicitly covers the g2 UV source files that were not individually listed in its flat old complete-certificate manifest. Source tables were not edited during computation. Exact rational mixture coefficients are exported in a separate JSON definition; no rounded binary64 NPZ is substituted for them.

The resulting proof extends over all even spins and all energies under the same stated physical assumptions, using existing analytic tails for the unrecomputed parts. It is internal shared-code interval certification with two precisions and exact accounting, not an independent external derivation or formal proof verification.

The corrected source-activity records are `head_l2_256_source_activity_repaired.json` and `head_l2_384_source_activity_repaired.json`, produced by `head/repair_mixture_activity.py`. No raw output or old proof input was changed.
