1. NORMALIZATION AND PHYSICAL ASSUMPTIONS
---------------------------------------
Set m=1, c=4/3, s+t+u=4, barred Mandelstam variables sbar=s-c, etc.

  a(s,t)=M(s,t)/(32*pi)
        =g0+g2*(sbar^2+tbar^2+ubar^2)+...

These are the unscaled manuscript massive couplings. All other EFT
coefficients remain free. The final optimization does not set them to zero.

  S_l=1+i*f_l, f_l=r_l+i*b_l,
  r_l^2+(b_l-1)^2<=1,
  rho(s)=sqrt(1-4/s),
  M(s,t)=(16*pi/rho(s))*sum_even_l (2l+1) f_l(s) P_l(1+2t/(s-4)),
  <F> = sum_even_l integral_4^infinity F_l(s) dnu_l(s),
  dnu_l(s)=(2l+1) ds/(2*pi*rho(s)).

Sources: paper Eq.(1), Eq.(23)-(24), paper.txt around lines49-57 and278-309.
The inequality allows inelasticity. Equality describes a purely elastic
partial wave; no elastic-only assumption was imposed.

Analytic hypotheses used by the nulls are important:
* A common real-analytic, crossing-symmetric identical-scalar amplitude,
  with the usual massive cut structure and a twice-subtracted fixed-transfer
  dispersion relation in the stated domain. The displayed formulas have no
  extra isolated-pole terms; amplitudes with such terms need their explicit
  subtraction/contribution before these equations can be reused.
* The even two-cut amplitude A_t(x) obeys A_t/x^2 ->0 on the deformed contour,
  endpoint contour contributions vanish, and weighted boundary integrals
  converge. Abel resummation requires corresponding weighted convergence
  and regularity (the paper gives absolute convergence/local Holder
  continuity as sufficient conditions).
* Partial-wave expansions and exchanges of spin sums, source integrals, and
  derivatives must converge appropriately. Transfer derivatives require
  locally uniform convergence. Independent unitarity disks do not imply
  these analytic/convergence facts.

Sources: Appendix A1 opening paragraph; A1.3 after(A1.7); A2 after(A2.2),
after(A2.4), and the validity-domain discussion after(A2.10); A5 after(A5.5).

The standard fixed-transfer range quoted by the paper is -28<t<4. The
simple symmetric two-cut harmonic coordinate requires t>-4. These are not
interchangeable. This implementation conservatively uses folded Roy sources
4<s<11.9, giving -3.95<t<=0, and high-energy fixed-transfer strips with
-4/3<=t<=0. The strips test the fixed-transfer dispersion relation above
s=12; they do not assert that the full Roy angular projection was extended
to s=48 or to infinite external energy. Spectral mu always ranges over
(4,infinity). No additional path-analyticity assumption from paper(A5.28)
was used in either final witness.

2. COUPLING KERNELS AND THE DUAL SUPPORT
-------------------------------------
For spectral energy mu define
  Delta=mu-4, Q=mu-c, Z=Q/Delta,
  Pstar_l=P_l(Z), p_l=Pstar_l/Q^3>0,
  F=32/(mu-2)^3,
  omega=((2+i*sqrt(mu*(mu-4)))/(mu-2))^2,
  C_l=8/[(3mu-8)(3mu-4)]*[1/(mu-2)-4Pstar_l/mu].
Then
  g2=<p_l*b_l>,
  g0=<u0_l*r_l+v0_l*b_l>,
  u0_l=F*Im(omega), v0_l=F*Re(omega)+C_l.

Sources: paper(A5.1),(A5.3); massive_search.py:39;
existing_solver/massive_forward_kernels.py:52.

Every stored row means N_a=<u_a*r+v_a*b>=0. A complex q kernel means
u=Im(q),v=Re(q), because Im(q*f)=Im(q)r+Re(q)b. No spectral measure is
included inside the returned kernel arrays.

For direction d=(cos(theta),sin(theta)) and real multipliers lambda_a,
  U=d0*u0+sum_a lambda_a*u_a,
  V=d0*v0+d1*p+sum_a lambda_a*v_a,
  d0*g0+d1*g2 <= D(lambda),
  D(lambda)=sum_even_l integral [sqrt(U_l^2+V_l^2)+V_l] dnu_l.
This is paper(A1.6), pointwise support of the shifted unit disk.
For V<0 numerical evaluation uses
  h=U^2/[hypot(U,V)-V],
not subtraction of nearly equal numbers. U and V are combined before taking
the support; supports of separate null rows must not simply be added.

The code sums normalized imaginary fields first:
  vnorm=V/p=d0*v0/p+d1+sum lambda_a*v_a/p,
then V=p*vnorm. This avoids cancellation between huge raw threshold kernels.
DiskSupport, dual_newton.py:17-51, implements the support, gradient and Hessian.
The normalized Roy/strip builders return u/sqrt(p),v/p, suitable for variables
R=sqrt(p)r and B=p*b; the raw builders return u,v. Do not mix these conventions.

Roy/strip builders initially produce

The actual stored homogeneous row is (u+gc*u0, v+gc*v0). This subtraction
elimination is implemented in search_problem.py:build_problem and
angle_constraints_experiment.py:build_rows, and independently in
angle_audit.py:normalized_inventory. Omitting gc changes the physical null.

6. RESUMMED FIXED-TRANSFER / ROY KERNELS
--------------------------------------
Sources: paper(A2.7)-(A2.9),(A5.30); roy_kernels.py:17,38,63,71,171;
transfer_kernels.py:9,39; test_transfer_audit.py.

At external(s,t), u=4-s-t, define
  K(mu;s,t;c)=1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)-1/(mu-c)-1/(mu-4+t+c).
The fixed-transfer equation is
  Re a(s,t)-g0=(1/pi) PV integral_4^infinity dmu
       [K(mu;s,t;c) Im a(mu,t)+K(mu;t,c;c) Im a(mu,c)].
Its combined spin kernel is
  L_l=P_l(y)K(mu;s,t;c)+Pstar_l K(mu;t,c;c),
  y=1+2t/Delta.
Every source row uses v_l=-integral_source(weight*L_l), with the appropriate
raw local real kernel u_l and subtraction coefficient gc.

Cancellation of the crossed-subtraction pole:
  d=mu-8/3+t,
  L_l=P_l(y)[1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)] +Pstar_l/(mu-t)
       -[P_l(y)+2Pstar_l]/Q +(Pstar_l-P_l(y))/d.
For even l, P_l(-Z)=Pstar_l, and
  (Pstar_l-P_l(y))/d=-(2/Delta) D_l(y,-Z),
  D_l(x,z)=[P_l(x)-P_l(z)]/(x-z).
Use the exact recurrence
  D0=0,D1=1,
  (l+1)D_(l+1)=(2l+1)[xD_l+P_l(z)]-lD_(l-1).
This is regular even when d=0; do not numerically subtract/divide there.

External energy PV cancellation in a folded Roy source:
  P_l(y)/(mu-s)=P_l(z)/(mu-s)+(1-z)/Delta*D_l(y,z),
where t=-(s-4)(1-z)/2. The remaining spectral PV is evaluated by the real
Legendre Q function
  Q_n(x)=1/2 PV integral_-1^1 P_n(v)/(x-v) dv.
For a Roy row the diagonal angular PV correction is
  4 Q_n(2(mu-4)/(B-4)-1)/[(2J+1)(B-4)] times delta_(l,J).
Energy nodes exactly at a source endpoint are rejected by real_legendre_q;
endpoint-split quadratures avoid evaluating those logarithmic formulas there.

The useful nonsingular UV identity is
  L_l/Pstar_l = L0 + Cuv*D_l(y,-Z)/Pstar_l,
  L0=[(s-c)^2/(mu-s)+(t-c)^2/(mu-t)+(u-c)^2/(mu-u)]/Q^2,
  Cuv=(2/Delta)*(s-c)*(c-u)*(2mu+t-4)/[(mu-s)Q(mu-u)].
In the UV, y+Z is bounded away from zero, so evaluate only desired spins with
  D_l(y,-Z)/Pstar_l=[P_l(y)/Pstar_l-1]/(y+Z).
The code now uses this faster quotient only in nonsingular UV branches;
low-energy removable-pole and PV branches retain the stable recurrence.

Folded Roy sources:
  w_n(s)=P_n(2(s-4)/(B-4)-1)/(B-4), 4<s<B=11.9,
  integral ds*w_n(s) integral_0^1 2dz*P_J(z) R(s,t(s,z))=0,
  t(s,z)=-(s-4)(1-z)/2.
All included J are even. The local raw real kernel is
  u_l(mu)=2*pi*w_n(mu)/(2J+1)*delta_(l,J),
and gc=-2 only for J=n=0, otherwise0. This factor2 is from the folded
angular measure. Before eliminating gc, real Roy kernels vanish above max J;
after elimination the gc*u0 contribution still exists at every spin.

High-energy tapered fixed-transfer sources:
  (A,B)=(12,24),(24,48), T=4/3,
  x=2(s-A)/(B-A)-1,
  w_n(s)=(1-x^2)P_n(x)/(B-A),
  h_j(t)=P_j(1+2t/T)/T, -T<=t<=0,
  integral_A^B ds*w_n(s) integral_-T^0 dt*h_j(t) R(s,t)=0.
For the untapered basis the local raw real kernel is
  u_l(mu)=pi*P_n(2(mu-A)/(B-A)-1)/(B-A)
                 *integral_-T^0 h_j(t)P_l(1+2t/(mu-4))dt,
supported A<mu<B. It affects ALL even spins, unlike a finite-J Roy source.
The untapered gc is-1 for j=n=0, otherwise0.

The tapered builder expands(1-x^2)P_n(x) in Legendre polynomials using
1-x^2=(2/3)(P0-P2). In the mathematical basis this is an exact finite
polynomial identity. The numerical legmul coefficients are binary64;
roundoff in an expanded coefficient sum must not be treated as a proof about
endpoint behavior. The intended basis is the exact product(1-x^2)P_n(x).
For each window its tapered subtraction coefficients are
  gc=-2/3 for j=0,n=0;
  gc=+2/15 for j=0,n=2;
  gc=0 otherwise.
The n=2 term is easy to miss. The implementation transforms gc correctly.
Endpoint vanishing removes the logarithmic endpoint spikes of an untapered
smearing weight. It does not certify every other tail contribution.

7. THRESHOLD-REGULAR TRANSFER DERIVATIVE ROWS

  KA=2/Qt-1/Q-1/(mu-8/3+t),
  KB=1/(mu-t)+1/(mu-8/3+t)-2/Q.

  q_(p,j)=d^p/dt^p [Ft P_l,t omega_t^j (1-omega_t)^(2p-2)] at t=0.
Then Dt{p}H_j{j} has u=Im q,v=Re q.

For Dt{p}G_regular define
  qG=d^p/dt^p [Ft P_l,t omega_t (1-omega_t)^(2p-2)] at0,
  u=Im qG,
  v=Re qG - d^p/dt^p [P_l,t KA+P_l,* KB] at0.
The subtraction term is NOT multiplied by(1-omega_t)^(2p-2). This is a
derivative of G_t plus a finite linear combination of higher harmonic nulls.
p=1 is G'_0; p=2 is the paper's Q2; p=3 is its Q3. Inserting the powers
before differentiation is essential for the threshold cancellations.

The implementation uses exact truncated Taylor algebra (convolution and
integer powers) in t, not finite differences. The stable identity
  1-omega_t=-2i sqrt((mu-4)(mu+t))
                 *(At+i sqrt((mu-4)(mu+t)))/Qt^2
avoids subtracting two nearly equal numbers at threshold. The Legendre
Taylor coefficients are
  [t^k] P_l,t=binom(l,k)binom(l+k,k)/(mu-4)^k.
65-digit independent mpmath differentiation checked p<=3,l=0,2,8 at
mu=4.01,5,12,100; relative-scale error about1.7e-13. That validates evaluation
of these formulas, not the amplitude's convergence hypotheses.

Raw negative-transfer harmonic point tests were tried but not retained:
their partial-wave factors can grow too fast at threshold and make the
pointwise disk support divergent. The regular derivative construction is
the retained alternative. The paper's separate path-continuation tests were
not added to either final state.

10. NECESSARY LARGE-SPIN SIGNS AND THE JOINT UV LIMIT
--------------------------------------------------
Sources: crossing_asymptotic_obstruction.txt, crossing_spin_probe.py,

At fixed mu>48, every source spectral Legendre argument is physical, so
|P_l(y)|<=1, while P_l(Z) grows exponentially since Z>1. All normalized

harmonics have polynomial spin factors times this exponential suppression.

The dangerous bands found by the auditor occur in joint mu~l^2 scaling.
For T_l=l(l+1), tau=T_l/(mu-4), c=4/3, define

  tail contribution to the target functional <=sqrt(2G A_L).
