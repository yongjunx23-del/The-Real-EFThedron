Provenance scope
================
The finite-A and all-spin-tail certificates have separately reviewed
analytic formulas and directed arithmetic runs. The assembly trusts those
component records, checks exact partitions/sums and the tail manifest, and
replays the head by default. It is a reproducible component assembly, not
a formal proof assistant or an independent derivation of the physical
amplitude assumptions. The mathematical claim remains conditional on the
stated manuscript dispersion, crossing, analyticity and unitarity assumptions.
