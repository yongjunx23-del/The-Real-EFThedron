4. ANGLE DEFINITION AND EXACT PLOT CONSTRUCTION

h(theta)=sup(g0*cos(theta)+g2*sin(theta)); theta is in degrees and uses
unscaled physical coupling axes. Theta is the supporting-line OUTWARD NORMAL
angle, not the polar angle of a boundary point, nor a plotting aspect angle.
0:maxg0;90:maxg2;180:-ming0;270:-ming2. The plot uses
  Delta(theta)=h_published_graphical(theta)-h_candidate(theta).
Positive Delta means numerically smaller support. Negative values are kept.

research/plot_angle_comparison.py builds the convex hull of manuscript blue
vertices plus(0,0), then clips it successively with accepted halfplanes
  cos(theta_i)*g0+sin(theta_i)*g2 <= offset_i.
It evaluates the support of the final polygon at1441 angles,0..360 inclusive
in0.25-degree steps. The orange curve is this INTERSECTION's support, not
linear interpolation of per-angle optimized objective values. The orange
region inherits the graphical manuscript baseline. It is a conditional
research enclosure, not a separately certified all-spin reconstruction.

Selection logic in load_audits():
  - Search the configured directories for *_audit.json.
  - Group replays of the same witness by saved SHA256 (fallback witness path).
  - For each witness use highest(lmax,n), rather than the most favorable
    quadrature value. This avoids choosing low quadrature underestimates.
  - Require audit status finite_head_audited_tail_unproved, finite support,
    no explicit manual exclusion, and no unresolved fractional endpoint.
  - A sole X4 row requires lambda_X4<=0 (necessary asymptotic condition).
  - The seven degree<=8 crossing rows require strictly negative leading
    combination32*lambda_X8_1+64*lambda_X8_2; if zero, next powers need review.
  - Other crossing inventories need explicit asymptotic review.
  - Add specified measured tail-band costs from angle_tail_additions.json.
  - At the same direction choose the candidate with the smallest padded
    support. A selected halfplane can still be nonbinding on the blue polygon.

Each nonexact cushion is
  max(2e-5,4*(observed same-witness head replay spread
             +abs(measured tail-band refinement difference))).
Head spread compares available same-lmax replays with n>=80 to the selected
finest one. Zero spread may mean only one replay exists, not zero error.
The exact zero-multiplier270-degree halfplane has zero cushion. These cushions
are plotting/convergence allowances, NOT rigorous integration errors or
upper bounds on unmeasured spin/energy contributions. Missing tail entries
in angle_tail_additions.json mean no measured addition, not a proven zero tail.
