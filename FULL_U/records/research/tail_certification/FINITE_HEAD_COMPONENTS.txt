Exact strip angular polynomial and real norm
-------------------------------------------
For transfer basis P_j(1+2t/c), t in[-c,0], c=4/3, put a=c/(mu-4).
The normalized physical angular projection is
 H_lj(a)=int_0^1 P_l(1-2av) P_j(1-2v) dv
        =sum_{k=j}^l (-1)^(k+j)(l+k)!/
          [(l-k)!(k-j)!(k+j+1)!] a^k.
This follows from the terminating Taylor series for P_l and the beta moment
int_0^1 v^k P_j(1-2v)dv=(-1)^j(k!)^2/[(k-j)!(k+j+1)!], zero if k<j.
All coefficients are exact rationals. The polynomial is composed with
(a_mid+x) and then evaluated on the radius interval, using Arb polynomial
composition; this retains cancellations and encloses the entire cell.

U_l is the exact forward harmonic+rational U plus pi/(B-A) times the
tapered energy polynomial times these angular projections. No local Roy U
survives for l>=8 because Jmax=6. U is integrated in beta=sqrt(1-4/mu),
mu=4/(1-beta^2), with dmu/rho=8/(1-beta^2)^2 dbeta. The endpoint-cancelled
forward density is used outside strip windows; clipped strip intervals
include zero when a cell crosses an endpoint. Since P_l(Z) decreases with
beta, 1/P_l(Z) at the right endpoint of each cell is a rigorous upper bound.
Positive interval upper Riemann sums cover beta in[0,1], including both
endpoints. The saved exact dyadic cell masses sum to an upper bound below1/4.

Finite V kernel assembly
------------------------
Let delta=mu-4 and
 R_l(t,delta)=delta^l P_l(1+2t/delta)
            =sum_{j=0}^l C_lj t^j delta^(l-j),
 C_lj=(l+j)!/[(l-j)!(j!)^2].
Rstar=R_l(c,delta)=delta^lP_l(Z), a positive polynomial fordelta>=0.
All normalized kernels are expressed with denominatorRstar, so the limit
mu->4 is finite or has a controlled sign without overflow.

The Roy PV part is an EXACT polynomial for l>Jmax=6:
 int W R_l(t,delta)/(mu-s)
 =sum_{j=0}^{l-1} C_lj sum_{a=0}^{l-j-1}
       delta^(l-j-1-a) M_s[j,a].
Indeed divide each delta^(l-j) bydelta-(s-4). The remainder is
(s-4)^l int_z W P_l(z), which vanishes by angular orthogonality. This does
not approximate or sample a principal-value integral.

The removable subtraction quotient is
 [Rstar-R_l(t,delta)]/(delta+c+t)
 =-sum_{j=1}^l C_lj delta^(l-j)
    sum_{a=0}^{j-1} t^(j-1-a)(-delta-c)^a.
The code combines all rational coefficients multiplying a given moment T[r]
and delta power BEFORE multiplying by its Arb ball. This exact grouping is
necessary: treating repeated moment balls independently before cancellation
would produce artificial large uncertainty.

The nonsingular denominators are geometric series, e.g.
 1/(mu-u)=sum_{k=0}^{N-1}(u+h)^k/(mu+h)^(k+1)+remainder,
 |remainder|<=q^N/[(mu+h)(1-q)], q=r_u/(mu+h).
N32 is used. After contraction with R_l the numerators are exact polynomials
with Arb coefficient enclosures. The t denominator is handled likewise.
The error uses the independently certified source absolute masses rounded
up: RoyAbs<5.618; each stripAbs<0.2401 (each is bounded by their combinedmass).
For physical arguments, |P_l(y)|/Pstar<=1 is sufficient for this remainder.
For low-energy Roy arguments use
 max_source |R_l(t,delta)|/Rstar
 <= |R_l(-79/20,delta)|/Rstar + delta^l/Rstar.
This includes both the physical interval maximum1 and the extreme source
argument; Legendre absolute maxima on a real interval occur at its exterior
endpoint or are bounded by1 on[-1,1].

All Roy rational terms are combined over the common denominator
Q*(mu+4)^N*(mu+2)^N BEFORE interval evaluation. The exact numerator degree
is at most l+2N-2. Its two larger powers vanish because the full crossed
kernel is O(mu^-3); geometric truncation errors are O(mu^(-N-1)) and cannot
change those leading coefficients forN>=3. The code checks the computed
leading coefficient balls contain zero, then removes these analyticallyzero
coefficients. This is an algebraic identity, not an inference from a small
floating number. Polynomial evaluation is centered for finite delta and
reversed in w=1/delta for the ultraviolet.

The crossing kernel is computed algebraically from Rstar. If derivatives
R_c,R_cc refer to the auxiliary c in R_l(c,delta), then
 X4/p=[Q R_cc-3R_c]/[2Q Rstar].
The coefficient is the exact-binary64 lambda_X4 of the witness.
The forward real part is evaluated using real Chebyshev polynomials and the
positive completed-square denominator for each rational cosine row, just as
in the independently reviewed real-kernel certificate.

Analytic tapered strip PV
-------------------------
The energy PV separates from its transfer polynomial. With
v=2(mu-A)/(B-A)-1, it is2/(B-A) times
 Qtilde_n(v)=1/2 PV int_-1^1 (1-z^2)P_n(z)/(v-z) dz
           =(1-v^2)Q_n(v)+v delta_n0+(1/3)delta_n1.
For|v|<1, Q_n=P_n(v)*log|(v+1)/(v-1)|/2-R_n(v), with polynomialR_n from the
standard Legendre recurrence. For|v|>1, the code expands(1-z^2)P_n into
Legendre polynomials and uses the real part of the type3 Acb Q function;
negative arguments use Q_n(-v)=(-1)^(n+1)Q_n(v). The expansion avoids
catastrophic cancellation between growing polynomials in the exterior.

Cells touchingv=1 usee=v-1 and
 (1-v^2)log|(v+1)/(v-1)|
 =-e(2+e)log(2+e)+(2+e)e log|e|.
The continuous e log|e| is enclosed by its endpoint values on|e|<1/4,
where it is monotone decreasing. v=-1 follows by parity. Therefore strip
endpoints are genuinely enclosed, not cut out or sampled away.

For mu>=100 the strip PV also has a fast convergent geometric expansion
in(s-s_mid)/(mu-s_mid). Then all three source denominators can be combined
and their numerator has degree at mostl+3N-2. The geometric residual is
included explicitly. Below this range the exact Qtilde formula is used.
