Polynomial normalization and finite PV residue
=============================================
Set delta=mu-4, c=4/3, Q=delta+2c,
 R_l(t,delta)=delta^l P_l(1+2t/delta)
            =sum_(j=0)^l C_lj t^j delta^(l-j),
 C_lj=(l+j)!/[(l-j)!(j!)^2], Rstar=R_l(c,delta).
Rstar has positive coefficients and remains strictly positive atdelta=0.
Dividing the source kernel byp=P_l(Q/delta)/Q^3 is exactly multiplication
by Q^3 delta^l/Rstar. This is the normalization used throughout.

The Roy real PV term is divided polynomially:
 R_l(t,delta)/(delta-sigma)
 =sum_(j<l) C_lj t^j sum_(a<l-j) sigma^a delta^(l-j-1-a)
    +R_l(t,sigma)/(delta-sigma), sigma=s-4.
At Roy kinematics t=-sigma(1-z)/2,
 R_l(t,sigma)=sigma^l P_l(z). The angular projection of the residue is
zero for every l>=8 because the fixed witness has only even J<=6 and
integral_0^1 P_J(z)P_l(z)dz=0. This is an exact orthogonality identity,
not a numerically small residue or discarded singular term.

The other removable denominator is cancelled exactly using evenl:
 R_l(-delta-c,delta)=R_l(c,delta)=Rstar.
Thus (Rstar-R_l(t,delta))/(delta+c+t) is a polynomial. Expanding this
quotient gives precisely the grouped ddcoef formula in the implementation.
Grouping rational coefficients by their shared moment before interval
evaluation improves cancellation without altering the identity.

Moment exactness and geometric remainders
=========================================
The moment builders use validated Gauss rules only on polynomials. Roy's
degree bounds are energy94 and angular77, below GL48's exactness95.
Strip bounds are energy38 and angular73, below GL40's exactness79.
Each resulting moment ball encloses the exact source integral. The
coefficients are exact binary64 rationals; row endpoints and rational
parameters are exact decimals; strip T=4/3 is exact.

For each expansion1/(D-q), |q|<=r<D,

The u-channel polynomial numerator contributes the extra ratio
 |P_l(1+2t/delta)|/P_l(Q/delta).
This is at most1 for every strip at all energies, and for Roy whenever

and monotonicity of P_l(x) forx>=1. The t-channel remainder has no such

Exact top-degree cancellations
===============================
Before normalization, the full source kernel is
 P_l(y)[1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)-1/Q]
 +P_l(Z)[1/(mu-t)-2/Q]
 -(P_l(y)-P_l(Z))/(mu-2c+t),
 y=1+2t/delta, Z=Q/delta.
It is O(mu^-3) at fixedl and bounded source variables. Themu^-1 terms
cancel. Themu^-2 coefficient is the sum of
 l(l+1)(t-c), s+t+u-3c=0, and -l(l+1)(t-c), hence alsozero.
N=32 geometric truncations preserve both leading cancellations.
Consequently the rational numerator degree is at mostl+2N-2 for Roy
and at mostl+3N-2 for the strip UV representation. Setting the two
leading enclosure coefficients exactly tozero is justified by these
analytic identities; an interval merely containingzero alone would not
justify it. The contains-zero checks are consistency tests.

The homogeneous UV evaluation assumes the retained polynomial degree
equals this target. This equality was checked independently for every
one of the51 source/spin polynomials. A later assertion now enforces it.

Strip endpoint handling
========================
For zeta=2(mu-A)/(B-A)-1, the tapered PV projection is
 Qtilde_n(zeta)=.5PV integral_-1^1 (1-x^2)P_n(x)/(zeta-x)dx
 =(1-zeta^2)Q_n(zeta)+zeta delta_(n0)+(1/3)delta_(n1).
Inside(-1,1), Q_n=P_n*.5log((1+zeta)/(1-zeta))-R_n.
Nearzeta=1 the singular product is rewritten exactly in terms of
e log|e|, e=zeta-1. On|e|<1/4 this function is continuous and strictly
decreasing, so its interval image is enclosed by its endpoint values,
includinge=0. The negative endpoint uses parity(-1)^(n+1). Outside
the cut, the exact finite Legendre expansion is evaluated with Arb's
type3 real Q_n. Thus the strip endpoint limits are included and finite.
Cells too broad for the endpoint formula trigger subdivision.
