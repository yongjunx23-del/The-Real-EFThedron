Definitions useful for checking the proof
----------------------------------------
Let d=mu-4, Q=mu-c, Z=Q/d, y=1+2t/d, and let z be the external physical Roy
angle. Physical l is even. Folded Roy sources have 4<=s<=119/10, 0<=z<=1,
t=-(s-4)(1-z)/2, and u=4-s-t. Strip sources have s in [12,24] or [24,48] and
-c<=t<=0. The full combined dispersion kernel is
  D=P_l(y)[1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)] + P_l(Z)/(mu-t)
       -[P_l(y)+2P_l(Z)]/Q +[P_l(Z)-P_l(y)]/(mu+t-2c).
Its divided difference removes the last apparent pole. Another useful form is
  D/P_l(Z) = D0 + K*(P_l(y)/P_l(Z)-1),
  D0=1/(mu-s)+1/(mu-u)+1/(mu-t)-3/Q,
  K=(s-c)(c-u)(2mu+t-4)/[(mu-s)Q(mu-u)(mu+t-2c)].
For l above the finite Roy source degree, the PV residue projected against the
source is zero. Subtracting P_l(z)/(mu-s) before quadrature is exact: all
source J are even and <=6, so folded Legendre orthogonality annihilates it for
l>=42. The regularized kernel is R=D-P_l(z)/(mu-s).

clears all denominators in the source polynomial, and the affine map from

Low energy: exceptional Roy region
---------------------------------
Split E={t<=-3/5, y<-1}. On E, W>=0 and |t|>d. Since s-4>=2|t|,
  s-mu >= g(d)=max(d,6/5-d)>0.
All factors in K except (mu-s)(mu+t-2c) are positive. Hence sign(K) is minus
the sign of h=mu+t-2c. For even l, P_l(y)-P_l(Z) has the same minus sign of h
when y<-1; thus K*(P_l(y)/P_l(Z)-1)>=0, including the removable h=0 limit.
Using |P_l(z)|<=1 gives
  R/P_l(Z) >= -(1+1/P_l(Z))/g(d)-3/Q.
Multiplication by -W reverses this into precisely the code's roy_pos upper
contribution. The exact interval minimum of g is 3/5 when a cell straddles
d=3/5, otherwise it is the smaller endpoint value. That implementation checks.

Low energy: remaining Roy region
--------------------------------
Outside E, either y lies in [-1,1], or t>-3/5 and y<-1. In the latter case
|y|/Z <=(6/5)/(8/3)=9/20. Pairing the real roots of even P_l gives
P_l(|y|)/P_l(Z)<=(|y|/Z)^l. With mu<=8, Z>=5/3, so uniformly
  |P_l(y)|/P_l(Z) <= e0_l=(9/20)^l+1/P_l(5/3).

The physical divided difference, divided by d*P_l(Z), is bounded by
  edd_l=(5/4)*l(l+1)*(9/20)^l +(5/6)*l(l+1)/P_l(5/3).
Three cases prove this conservative expression:
* d>=3/5: the remaining y is physical, so |dd|<=l(l+1)/2 and 1/(2d)<=5/6.
* 2/5<=d<3/5: put Y=6/(5d)-1>=1. The derivative maximum on [-Y,Y] is P'_l(Y),
  and P'_l(Y)<=l(l+1)P_l(Y)/2. The root-pairing ratio bound and 1/(2d)<=5/4
  give the first term.
* d<2/5: P'_l(Y)/P_l(Y)<=l/sqrt(Y^2-1). After division by d this is at most
  (5/sqrt(12))*l*(9/20)^l, which is dominated by the first term for l>=42.
The logarithmic derivative bound follows, for example, by differentiating
Laplace's positive integral representation of P_l(x) for x>1.

The rest of the regular kernel, excluding K2, is
  (1-z)/d*dd(P_l;y,z)/P_l(Z)
  +P_l(y)/P_l(Z)*[1/(mu-u)-1/Q-1/(mu+t-2c)].
On this region h>=11/15, mu-u>=4, Q>=8/3, giving an absolute coefficient bound
1/4+3/8+15/11<2. Thus edd_l+2e0_l suffices, matching the code.

Only negative W can make the positive K2 part contribute positively to V.
Negative W lies in t>-3/5, so |t-c|<=29/15 and Q>=8/3. Consequently
  Q^3 K2 <=2*(29/15)^2*(8/3)^2/[(8/3)^2-(29/15)^2],
which matches k2neg. Source absolute/positive/negative norms are exact rational
Bernstein upper integrals, so this handles source sign changes safely.

Low energy strips and all-spin closure
-------------------------------------
Here mu<8<12<=s, and t>=-c implies |P_l(y)|<=P_l(Z). Also K<=0, so positive
strip weights satisfy D/P_l(Z)>=-1/(12-mu)-3/Q. For negative strip weights,
the remaining divided difference contributes at most
  2l/sqrt(Q^2-d^2).
This follows from its derivative bound P'_l(Z)/P_l(Z)<=l/sqrt(Z^2-1).
The other terms are bounded by 1/(12-mu)+2/mu+1/Q. These are exactly the
linear-in-l strip and constant strip terms in the code.

The crossing coercivity bound is
  X4/p >= l(2l-5)/Q^2, for even l>=4.
To verify it, write P_l(Z)=constant*product_i(Z^2-a_i^2), b_i=Z^2/(Z^2-a_i^2)
and m=l/2. Then [2Z^2 P''-3ZP']/P=8*sum_{i!=j}b_i b_j-2*sum_i b_i. For m>=2
it increases in each b_i>=1; its minimum is l(2l-5). This proof fixes both
the factor 2 and the sign of the negative X4 multiplier.

All other l-dependent positive terms can be replaced by their l=L values:
l(l+1)*(9/20)^l decreases for l>=42, and l(l+1)/P_l(5/3) does too. The code
then explicitly checks that the derivative of C1*l-eta*l(2l-5)/Q^2 is negative
at L. Since that derivative decreases further with l, this establishes the
low-energy bound for every l>=L, not merely the first omitted spin.

Middle energy: 8<=mu<=49
-----------------------
All Roy/strip physical arguments y are now in [-1,1]. Decompose
  Q^3 D/P_l(Z) = Q^3 K1*P_l(y)/P_l(Z)+Q^3 K2,
with the Roy PV residue removed as above. Since |t-c|<=317/60<Q,
  Q^3 K2=sum_{k>=0}2(t-c)^(2k+2)/Q^(2k).
The exact signed source moments are computed from elementary unit-square
monomial integrals. For Roy terms the y integral is the beta function
j!r!/(j+r+1)!, and the energy integral is 1/(i+r+1). For strips it is the
ordinary monomial integral of (2-y)^(2k+2). Both formulas in compact_source_data
match these integrals. The omitted series is bounded by
  norm2*(317/(60Q))^(2N)/[1-(317/(60Q))^2].
This is a true geometric remainder with N=40, not an extrapolation.

For Roy rows below mu=12, the subtracted pole is bounded by l(l+1)/(2d),
using the physical derivative bound. Above 12, mu-s>=mu-119/10>0 permits the
ordinary pole bound. The remaining denominators are bounded using
mu-u>=mu and h>=mu-397/60. The cell grid splits at mu=12, so each expression
is used only in its valid region.

For strips let h(x) be the normalized-energy source polynomial, x in [0,1].
The taper gives h(0)=h(1)=0 and
  sup|h'| <= sum_rows |lambda|[4+n(n+1)]=D_window.
PV integral_0^1 h(x)/(v-x) dx is uniformly at most 2D_window in absolute value:
subtract h(v), bound the difference quotient by D_window, and bound the log
term using |h(v)|<=D_window*min(v,1-v). Outside [0,1], endpoint vanishing gives
the even smaller D_window bound. Dividing by the physical window width yields
the code's 2D_window/(B-A). The other strip denominators are bounded by
1/mu+1/Q+1/d. No unresolved endpoint/PV divergence is being discarded.

All-spin monotonicity for this middle bound is important: for Z>1 the recurrence
implies P_(n+1)(Z)/P_n(Z)>=Z. On mu<=49,
Z>=143/135>(L+2)/L for L>=42. Meanwhile T_(n+1)/T_n=(n+2)/n. Therefore both
1/P_l(Z) and T_l/P_l(Z) decrease for all l>=L. This justifies evaluating the
PV numerator's T and denominator's P at L together. The negative crossing
term strengthens as l increases. The code's middle bound is therefore uniform.

Updated UV cofactor and harmonic errors

For R1-1, the numerator is exactly

  =(2-3t/2)mu^2+(-t^2-su+16t/3-32/9)mu
      +c^2(t-4)/2-su(t-2c).
On the stated source rectangle, the absolute coefficients are bounded by
8, 2153, and 14088 respectively. The denominator divided by mu^3 is bounded
below by (1-48/M)(1-20/(3M)). Thus the new rational cofactor error is valid;
taking its minimum with the previous valid error is safe.

The common harmonic prefactor rf multiplies the entire limiting combination
-alpha+sum_j lambda_j*(-1)^j. Taking the absolute value after forming that
combination is legitimate; differences away from the limiting phases are
still separately bounded by the existing j^2 Chebyshev errors. No cancellation
between unrelated unknown-sign remainders is assumed.

Newest integrated-t UV correction
---------------------------------
For every positive Bernstein coefficient, the product basis supplies an exact
positive measure. In a source box, its mean energy coordinate is
a+(b-a)(i+1)/(n+2) and its mean transfer coordinate is
d+(e-d)(j+1)/(m+2). Roy |t| is bilinear in these independent coordinates and
strip |t| is affine. The new source_data lambda computes their exact means.
Summing coefficient masses times these means bounds the positive-source
|t|-moment. Therefore the correction is bounded by the smaller of twice the
positive mass and tau times the positive |t|-moment. This is precisely the
new min(2*mass,tau*moment) formula. It improves the previous box maximum-|t|
formula without changing the domain. Its cache is versioned separately.
