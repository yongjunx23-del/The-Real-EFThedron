## Centered and fourth-order normalized enclosures

For a cell with midpointm and radiush, the second-order centered enclosure is
f(m)+[-h|f'(m)|-h^2 sup|f''|/2, h|f'(m)|+h^2 sup|f''|/2].
The fourth-order version retains the exact midpoint derivatives through
order3 and bounds the fourth derivative over the whole cell. The Taylor
coefficients of Arb series already include the factorial denominators.
These are applied to the real field U/e^2 and the normalized imaginary
field V/p. Source-moment geometric errors are uniform value errors added
after the Taylor enclosure; they are not differentiated as constant balls.

`finite_quartic.py` replaces a process-local polynomial series helper so it
preserves the caller's requested series precision5 instead of truncating to3.
No old source file is edited. The inner transfer-derivative expansion still
has order3 because the physical derivative rows have orders1..3; the outer
beta derivatives through order4 are a different series variable.

Where V/p is proved negative, H(U,V)<=U^2/(-2V) gives an interval support
bound with the exact positive Legendre normalization. Else the independent
raw-field Riemann/convex-interpolation upper bound remains available.
The smaller of independently valid bounds is selected. The normalized
method covers cells below beta0.99; the original factored UV enclosure
covers the remainder through beta1, including infinite energy.

Principal-value evaluation is deliberately held to one expression on each
Taylor cell. The full interval chooses either the geometric polynomial or
the exact polynomial/log expression; the midpoint uses that same choice.
Otherwise a midpoint derivative and an interval remainder could refer to
different approximants, which would not justify a Taylor certificate.
`finite_global.py` records this choice in `_pv_modes`.

Source activity in the real field is determined from exact rational beta
endpoints. Cells crossing a source endpoint are handled by separate gap
certificates, not by extrapolating an active/inactive branch through it.

## Finite threshold neighborhoods

The true derivative real field behaves as1/beta for positive spin. The code
encloses beta*U/e^2 and integrates the explicit power of beta in1/p.
For spins2,4 the normalized imaginary field is split into an even analytic
part and explicit beta^(2ell)*log(beta) source terms. A directed bound on
the second derivative proves a negative constant plus a negative beta^2
coefficient on beta in[0,1/256]. Higher spins use a negative constant margin.

This is a finite-width statement, unlike the prior threshold-limit checks.
The cap mass and sign coefficients are included in each component record.

## High-spin source-endpoint caps

The old absolute bound on a gap, integral(|U|+2|V|), was valid but enormous
at higher spin because it ignored a large negative V. It was replaced in
`finite_gap.py` for ell>6 by the following rigorous normalized cap.

There is no local Roy real/PV source polynomial when ell>6, since the row
bank has J<=6. The code verifies that the relevant polynomial is identically
zero. Each strip polynomial has the exact factorization
W(y)=(1-y^2)P(y). Remove its PV logarithm from the analytic enclosure and
bound the removed term uniformly using

```
|W(y) log |(y+1)/(y-1)||
 <= |P(y)| [ |y+1| sup |(y-1)log|y-1||
           +|y-1| sup |(y+1)log|y+1|| ].
```

On a small interval containing zero, sup|x log|x|| is r|log r|, where
|x|<=r<0.1. This includes the continuous zero value at the endpoint.
The exact normalized prefactor is Q^3 H_ell/(width Rstar).
The untapered polynomial P is rebuilt from exact rational multipliers and
Legendre polynomials, rather than inferred by dividing rounded coefficients.

The real strip source is enclosed with the active branch united with zero
when the gap crosses its support endpoint. If the completed normalized
V enclosure has negative upper endpoint, integrate U^2/(-2V) over the
whole gap. If negativity cannot be proved, fall back to the original valid
absolute log-integral bound. No endpoint is excluded without a bound.

For this witness the four source-gap sums at spin32 and40 are respectively
below7.079e-13 and3.012e-14. The previous absolute gap estimates were about
74960 and2.15e8. This change is an enclosure improvement, not a change to the
functional or a removal of any constraint.
