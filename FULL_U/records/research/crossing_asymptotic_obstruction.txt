Necessary large-spin sign conditions for the searched dual witnesses
==================================================================

Fix a spectral energy mu>48, and write Delta=mu-4, Q=mu-4/3,
Z=Q/Delta>1, p_l=P_l(Z)/Q^3. This avoids every smearing endpoint and
every external principal-value pole. All Legendre arguments in the
Roy source (B=11.9) and transfer strips are in [-1,1]. Their physical
Legendre factors are bounded by one; after division by P_l(Z), only
finite subtraction terms survive. The local real Roy coefficients are
zero above the highest source J. Finite forward derivatives add powers
of l but remain exponentially suppressed after division by P_l(Z).
The rational/fractional forward nulls have the same suppression.

For the X4 convention in crossing_kernels.py,

  X4_l/p_l = [2 Z^2 P_l''(Z)-3 Z P_l'(Z)]/[Q^2 P_l(Z)]
           = 2 Z^2 l^2/[Q^2 (Z^2-1)] + O(l).

The coefficient of l^2 is strictly positive. Therefore a witness whose
only crossing row is X4 necessarily requires lambda_X4<=0. If lambda_X4
is positive, V_l/p_l tends to +infinity at every fixed mu>48, and hence
on an open spectral interval. Since p_l grows exponentially in l,
the disk support sum diverges. No finite spin cutoff can rescue it.
This is only a necessary condition: a negative lambda_X4 does not prove
the sign in the intermediate-spin or joint mu,l scaling regions.

More generally the beta_j metadata in crossing_catalogue multiplies

  (2^j/j!) P_l^(j)(Z) / [Delta^j Q^(D+1-j)]

before division by p_l. The leading derivative ratio is
P_l^(j)(Z)/P_l(Z)=l^j/(Z^2-1)^(j/2)+O(l^(j-1)).
The highest j among included crossing rows therefore supplies a necessary
sign restriction. Through degree eight, j=6 occurs only at degree eight,
with beta_6=32 in X8_1 and beta_6=64 in X8_2. Thus

  32 lambda_X8_1 + 64 lambda_X8_2 <= 0

is necessary. If this combination vanishes exactly, the next asymptotic
power must be examined; a strictly negative margin is preferable in
numerical search. Positive leading coefficient cannot be compensated by
the exponentially small forward derivative nulls or bounded normalized
Roy/strip subtraction terms.

angle_constraints_experiment.py now imposes the highest-degree sign
condition and supplements source/threshold guards with a dense moderate
energy grid through spin60 and additional spins64,80,100,140,200.
These restrictions constrain the searched witness coefficients; they
are not added physical assumptions and do not themselves certify a
continuum all-spin support bound.
