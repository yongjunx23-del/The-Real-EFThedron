Family and exact signs
----------------------
We bound g0+b*g2, with homogeneous X4 multiplier increment d. The agreed
family is d=0 for b<=0, and d=4*b for b>0. All family parameters are exact
rationals; the base witness multipliers retain their exact binary64 values.
No modified floating-point witness needs to be saved or rounded.

Let delta=mu-4, c=4/3, Q=mu-c, Z=1+2c/delta,
 p_l=P_l(Z)/Q^3>0 and dnu_l=(2l+1)dmu/(2*pi*rho(mu)).
The exact transformed kernels are
 U_new=U_old,
 V_new/p=V_old/p+b+d*chi_l,  chi_l=X4_l/p.
Because U is unchanged, all old real-norm estimates remain valid.

Writing R_l(c,delta)=delta^l P_l(1+2c/delta),
 chi_l=(Q*d_c^2 R_l-3*d_c R_l)/(2*Q*R_l).
For l=2 this is -6/(3Q^2-delta^2), tending to-9/32 at threshold and0-
in the UV. Thus d=4b gives b+d*chi_2 ->-b/8 at threshold for b>0.
For every even l>=4, chi_l>=l(2l-5)/Q^2>0, as proved in the old tail
certificate. Positive d therefore weakens higher-spin coercivity: one
must replay the analytic envelope with the LESS NEGATIVE full X4
coefficient. Adding d times a LOWER chi bound to the old V upper is NOT
a valid upper bound. The new code never makes that incorrect substitution.

All-spin tail
-------------
certify_directional_tail.py reuses the old exact source data and the same
compact/UV inequalities. certify_tail_4b.py selects the agreed positive
repair and writes tail_family_4b.json. The older tail_family.json is only
the exploratory d=(32/9)b family and is not used with root's d=4b heads.

Negative b,d0 reuse the old global bound directly:
 V_new/p<=-(31/50-b).
Positive b recompute the compact envelope with lambda_X4+d<0, and in the
UV use source_upper+(lambda_X4+d)*certified_chi_lower+b. The added b is
exact. Low/middle-energy bounds retain the large-spin derivative check;
the UV calculation includes all tau through infinity with the same
positive-coefficient Legendre bounds and large-tau closure. Source norms,
signed moments, and the dangerous Roy-source positivity certificate are
unchanged and all their hashes are verified.

For all even l>=42 and every mu>4, tail_family_4b.json reports PASS for
 b=-1,-1/2,-1/4,-1/10,0,1/10,1/4.
Using the old certified A40 and h(U,V)<=U^2/(2*kappa*p), the respective
tail support upper values are approximately
 6.17272543e-6,8.92840643e-6,1.14940405e-5,1.38886323e-5,
 1.61287342e-5,2.35909932e-5,7.94991363e-5.
Use the exact rational fields for assembly, not these approximate values.
The b=1/2,d2 envelope fails. This is a failure of this sufficient envelope,
not a proof that the candidate is infeasible, and no complete bound is
claimed for that direction.

Per-spin finite block
---------------------
certify_finite_vectors.py has two read-only producer modes:
 real: per-spin upper masses on the old 19,790-cell beta partition,
       including threshold and UV endpoints exactly as in FiniteReal.
 v: fresh Arb280 enclosures of V_old/p and chi_l for each spin on every
       spectral leaf. Starting from the old 8,798-leaf partition, six
       additional splits yield8,804 leaves. This covers mu in[4,101]
       and w=1/(mu-4) in[0,1/97], with all17 even spins8..40.

VectorReal.densities is the term-by-term spin decomposition of the
reviewed scalar FiniteReal.density. The old inverse-Legendre monotonic
endpoint bound, exact strip angular polynomial, endpoint clipping, and
UV-cancelled forward formula are unchanged. The V producer imports the
reviewed finite-moment evaluator; its X4 formula above and reversed w
polynomial are explicit rational/Arb expressions.

The producer's certified_cases metadata lists d=(32/9)b pilot cases used
only to guide refinement. Its raw per-spin V_upper and X4_upper columns
are valid independent of that list. combine_finite_family.py explicitly
checks the actual d=4b cases against these raw columns before using them.
Since d>=0, the exact rational value
   V_upper+b+d*X4_upper
is a valid upper bound; this would not suffice for d<0 without X4_lower.

The merger converts all beta and reciprocal-energy endpoints to exact
rational mu endpoints, with beta1/w0 interpreted as infinity. It verifies
gapless partitions. For each spin and beta cell I, it uses
 k_{l,I}=min over EVERY intersecting spectral leaf of
          -(V_upper+b+d*X4_upper)>0.
If M_{l,I} bounds integral_I U_l^2/p_l dnu_l, then
 integral_I h(U_l,V_l) dnu_l <= M_{l,I}/(2*k_{l,I}).
All comparisons, intersections, and minima use exact Fractions; only
outward-rounded division and summation use Arb280. Every nonfinite or
nonpositive-coercivity case is rejected explicitly.

Assembly and normalization
--------------------------
These components alone are not complete support bounds. Add independently
certified spin0,2,4,6 contributions for the SAME exact(b,d), and require
PASS for both finite block and tail. Root owns that assembly.
The result is a bound on g0+b*g2. To express a unit-normal support function
at theta=atan(b), divide bysqrt(1+b^2). Do not confuse b with the angle.
