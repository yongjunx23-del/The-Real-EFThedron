Scope and answer
----------------
The relevant 2021 paper is Guerrieri and Sever, "Rigorous bounds on the Analytic
S-matrix", arXiv:2106.10257v2, PRL 127, 251601. The other paper is Elias Miro,
Guerrieri and Gumus, "The Phases of the Scalar S-Matrix Island", 2605.06613v1.
The somewhat different title supplied in the request appears to refer to the
former; I did not identify a separate primary paper under that exact title.

Three distinct claims must remain separate:
1. An exact feasible dual functional bounds every amplitude in the stated
   axiomatic class. Finite-dimensional parametrization of that dual functional
   is legitimate; it restricts the search, not the amplitudes.
2. A numerical witness supported by sampled inequalities, analytic leading
   asymptotics and convergence studies has the numerical evidence described in
   these papers. That evidence is stronger than an unaudited finite-spin fit.
3. A continuum certificate additionally closes every omitted spin/energy range
   and bounds numerical errors. Neither inspected paper documents an interval
   arithmetic, directed-rounding, machine-checkable certificate for its quoted
   optimal decimal numbers. Do not attribute that stronger standard to them.

An optimizer's finite-grid success does not establish claim 1 for a saved
floating-point witness. Conversely, no theorem requires interval arithmetic
specifically: exact analytic inequalities and a rigorously bounded integral
would also suffice. Known violations cannot be ignored because a paper uses
the word rigorous.

AND ODD source indices. Physical partial-wave spins l remain even. The original
source angular projection is over [-1,1]; even sources can be folded to [0,1].
Sec II.B, Eq (21): coefficient of g0 is 1-2*integral(w0).
Eq (22): bar_w_l(v) is the source integral of the Roy kernel.
Eqs (23)-(24): finite-head objective and 2-by-2 positive-semidefinite matrices.

spin and EVERY spectral energy v>4, including v above the source endpoint.
Eq (26): integral(w0)=1/2.
App B, Eqs (B4)-(B6): setting the complementary epigraph function X_l to zero
is allowed only after imposing those sign conditions.

Thus the upper source limit 12 is NOT an ultraviolet cutoff on the physical
amplitude. Its purpose is to restrict the dual source. The spectral dispersion
integrals still extend to infinity. The objective has an exactly zero tail
only for a source satisfying the complementary conditions.

2021 large-spin and threshold mechanism
--------------------------------------
App E (PDF E1-E14; HTML 54-67) is the all-spin analytic guide:
  E1-E2: even source s<=mu_e^2, odd source s<=mu_o^2, angular domains 0..1 and
         -1..1 respectively. The odd term carries 1/2 relative to their folded
         kernel convention, not an invariant normalization of an odd null.
  E3: leading large-l Legendre asymptotic at fixed argument x>1.
  E4-E6: competition of two exponential terms; if
         mu_o^2=2+mu_e^2/2, the transition is
         v_c=2+mu_e^2/2-t0. With t0=4/3 and mu_e^2=12, v_c=20/3.
         For our B_e=11.9, v_c=6.616666666666667 and paired B_o=7.95.
  E7-E9: outer region v>v_c factors a positive Legendre term times ybar(v),
         independent of physical spin. Its required sign is imposed on a
         grid in v in the published implementation.
  E10-E12: inner region 4<v<v_c is controlled at leading large spin by endpoint
         source values and a fixed-sign rational kernel.
  E13-E14: the kernel-sign argument gives the practical endpoint restrictions
         mu_e^2<=12 and mu_o^2<=8 and an endpoint linear condition.
App F, Eqs F1-F3 (HTML 68-70): leading threshold powers yield additional linear
sign constraints for intermediate spins beyond the main J_max, up to a further
finite spin cutoff.

For implementation use the endpoint bracket directly from E12:
  sum_n w_(2n)(B_e) P_(2n)(0)
  - 1/2 sum_n w_(2n+1)(2+B_e/2) P_(2n+1)(-1).
The source's E14 gamma-expression seems to omit the 1/sqrt(pi) in
P_(2n)(0)=(-1)^n Gamma(n+1/2)/(sqrt(pi)*Gamma(n+1)); copying it literally can
change relative even/odd normalization. Re-derive the bracket in the chosen
kernel normalization. Also a sentence near E3 says t0=3/4, while the actual
subtraction point throughout the construction is 4/3. These are reproduction
warnings, not a claim that the numerical code used those apparent typos.

Our inference about completeness: leading fixed-v asymptotics do not by
themselves furnish a computable uniform finite l_* beyond which a sign holds.
The transition v approximately v_c, source endpoint zeros, and joint limits
v of order l^2 require separate treatment. An inequality that is eventually
true at each fixed v can still fail along a sequence v(l). In this project
the scaled-energy audits already found exactly that kind of omitted region.
Any claimed continuum certificate must close it analytically or by a proved
uniform enclosure. Sampling larger isolated spins alone does not do so.

2021 numerical implementation: concrete settings and limitations
---------------------------------------------------------------
App G, Eq G1 (HTML 71): Chebyshev energy polynomials for source w_j; polynomial
epigraph X_j with an additional alpha_0/sqrt(s-4) threshold term. NX=40 is an
example; even Nw was varied through 6..20, odd Nw fixed at 4. Source spin L=0,
2,4 for upper g0 and up to 6 for lower g0. Kernel-source integrals were evaluated
analytically using elementary functions, logs and dilogs. Head 4<v<12 and UV
12<v<infinity were mapped separately to [-1,1], with 500 and 80 Chebyshev grid
points. Further refined-grid X_j>=0 tests were used; a Bernstein alternative
is mentioned in footnote 18 but found to converge more slowly.

Sec II.C and App G.1: the upper number 2.7272 is an Nw-to-infinity power-law
extrapolation. The Fig 4 caption's L-to-infinity wording conflicts with the
prose, which explicitly says Nw. They report no additional high-spin violations
after a sufficient cutoff for that maximum problem.
Sec II.C and App G.2: the lower number -8.08 uses L=6, Nw=20 and extrapolation
in J_max; the difference from the highest quoted J_max=26 value is 0.004.
The text explicitly acknowledges small remaining violations at J>J_max in
intermediate outer-region energies that depend on J. This is especially
relevant to our lower-g0 audit. It is not a numerical interval certificate of
the saved finite-cutoff witness. The authors nonetheless describe their bounds
as rigorous numerical bounds; report both the claim and the disclosed method.

The threshold singularity in G1 belongs to an objective epigraph, not to a
fractional holomorphic equality kernel. It therefore does not validate our
discarded singular fractional-bank witnesses or their inadequate quadrature.

Exact mapping to this repository's dual variables
-----------------------------------------------
This section is an independent derivation from our checked kernels. Define
beta(v)=sqrt(1-4/v), r_l=beta*Re(f_l), b_l=beta*Im(f_l). Then
  r_l^2+(b_l-1)^2 <= 1,
  dnu_l=(2*l+1)dv/(2*pi*beta),
  g2=sum_l integral dnu_l p_l b_l,
and the local support is H(U,V)=V+sqrt(U^2+V^2). U,V here are RAW physical
kernels, not the internally scaled U/sqrt(p), V/p arrays.

For an objective a*g0+b*g2 and null multipliers lambda_i,
  U=a*u0+sum_i lambda_i*u_i,
  V=a*v0+b*p+sum_i lambda_i*v_i.
The exact dual value is the infinite-spin, full-energy integral of H. For
V<0 use H=U^2/(sqrt(U^2+V^2)-V) to avoid cancellation. A finite-grid integral is
only an approximation of part of this object unless the complement is closed.

The Roy bank returns a source row (uRoy,vRoy,c_i) for the equality
  c_i*g0 + integral dnu (uRoy*r+vRoy*b)=0.
massive_search.py replaces g0 by the forward formula, yielding the stored row
  u_i=uRoy+c_i*u0, v_i=vRoy+c_i*v0.
In build_roy_bank, c_i=-2 only for J=0,n=0, and c_i=0 otherwise.
Consequently, a paper-style compact real kernel is obtained by:
  (i) using finite-J Roy sources and absorptive-only crossing nulls;
  (ii) setting other all-spin real-kernel multipliers to zero;
  (iii) imposing the affine equality a+sum_i lambda_i*c_i=0.
For a single B bank this is lambda_Roy_B11.9_J0_n0=a/2. With several B banks,
the sum of their J0,n0 multipliers is a/2. At max/min g0 the required sum is
+1/2 or -1/2 respectively. This is a restriction on the dual search, so is
safe; it is not an extra physical assumption.

Then U is supported only at the even source spins and 4<v<max(B). Outside,
H(0,V)=2*max(V,0); a proved V<=0 makes the entire complement exactly zero.
Absorptive crossing nulls can improve V without enlarging U. Additional odd
Roy sources described next have the same advantage. Polynomial Legendre and
Chebyshev energy bases span the same finite-dimensional polynomial space.

Precise odd-source construction and normalization
------------------------------------------------
Sec II.A Eq 19 and its following paragraph explicitly note that odd source
projections contain no real part, because all physical odd partial waves are
zero. This is a nontrivial crossing identity of the fixed-t representation.
It is NOT obtained by inserting odd J into our folded angular integrator.

Let c=4/3, d=v-4, Q=v-c, Z=Q/d, t=-(s-4)(1-z)/2, u=4-s-t,
x=1+2t/d. For even physical l, let D_l be the existing combined Roy integrand:
  D_l=P_l(x)[1/(v-s)+1/(v-u)]+P_l(Z)/(v-t)
      -[P_l(x)+2P_l(Z)]/(v-c)
      +[P_l(Z)-P_l(x)]/(v-8/3+t).
The last quotient is removable and is evaluated as
  -2/d * divided_difference(P_l; x,-Z).
Choose an odd SOURCE J, energy w_n(s)=P_n(2(s-4)/(B_o-4)-1)/(B_o-4), and set
  U_odd(l,v)=0, c_odd=0,
  V_odd(l,v)=-integral_4^B_o ds w_n(s)
                 *integral_-1^1 dz P_J(z) D_l(s,z;v).
This is a raw absorptive-only null in OUR dnu normalization. Multiplying an
entire null by a fixed constant changes only its multiplier convention.

There is NO extra 1/2 to insert when implementing it beside roy_kernels.py:
the existing even angular GL weights represent 2*dz on [0,1], whereas the new
odd angular GL weights represent dz on [-1,1]. Each rule's weights sum to 2.
The paper's E1 factor 1/2 compensates its doubled folded-kernel prefactor;
it must not be applied a second time to our raw full-domain construction.

The external PV pole has residue integral_-1^1 P_J(z)P_l(z)dz=0. Thus replace
  P_l(x)/(v-s) -> (1-z)/d * divided_difference(P_l; x,z)
inside the odd projected integral and add NO Legendre-Q PV term. The omitted
piece P_l(z)/(v-s) integrates to zero exactly for odd J/even l. This removes
the pole before quadrature and is preferable to numerical cancellation.
In normalized arrays return v=-Q^3*(source/angular integral of D_l/P_l(Z));
u remains identically zero. Enforce l=0 row zero analytically as a useful
consistency check: its full integrand is even under z->-z. Further useful tests
are angular-order convergence, agreement with direct safe off-PV integration,
and vanishing on a crossing-symmetric pole-exchange amplitude.

Validity vs useful endpoint: the full projection fits the fixed-t domain for
4<B_o<32 because t>=4-B_o>-28. The paper's generic endpoint/asymptotic feasible
source family uses B_o<=8. For matching their common transition to B_e=11.9,
choose B_o=7.95; B_o=7.9 is another conservative finite-source choice but its
inner/outer transition differs. Restricting B_o is not a truncation of v.
The source function can be tapered at its endpoint, but then its leading
large-spin endpoint formula must be re-derived at the first nonzero derivative.

How to certify an existing rich witness instead
---------------------------------------------
The resummed forward, rational, strip, and many derivative real kernels in our
saved witnesses generally survive at all physical spins. V<=0 on their tail
does not make H vanish when U is nonzero. Their certification therefore needs
an actual positive remainder estimate, or a redesign with the compact-U
equality above. A sampled negative Roy source screen is a useful obstruction
diagnostic; a positive source screen alone proves neither full V<=0 nor a
bound on this nonzero-U remainder.

Two rigorous sufficient estimates, if their hypotheses can be proved, are:
  A_R = sum_l integral_R dnu_l U_l^2/p_l.
  If V_l<=-delta*p_l throughout R for a PROVED delta>0, then
       integral_R H(U,V) dnu <= A_R/(2*delta).
  If only V<=0 is known, but an INDEPENDENT certified g2<=G is available,
       contribution_R to the physical linear functional <=sqrt(2*G*A_R).
The second statement uses r^2<=2b and Cauchy-Schwarz; it bounds the functional
on physical amplitudes with the additional global g2 information, not the
unconstrained integral of local supports. Do not mistake it for an inequality
on integral(H) absent that global constraint. Both estimates need a finite,
rigorously controlled A_R, including high energy and all omitted spins.

Practical completion plan for a numerical/analytic certificate
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Freeze one explicit finite vector of coefficients and its precise kernel
   definitions. Optimization need not itself be exact; verification must use
   the frozen vector. Coefficient rounding can be absorbed with error bounds
   or the coefficients can be treated as exact binary/rational numbers.
2. Separate physical-spin head, threshold cells, bounded energy cells, UV,
   transition v_c, and joint v~l^2 region. Source-energy integration errors
   inside every Roy kernel are additional errors, not the same as objective
   quadrature error. Analytic PV subtraction and endpoint variables are vital.
3. Prove complementary V signs for compact U, or integrate/bound the actual
   support for rich U. Use strict sign margins and a uniform remainder in
   large-spin asymptotics; the exact transition and any zeros need their own
   enclosures. A large number of grid points is not a substitute for margins
   plus derivative/interval control between points.
4. On finite spin/energy cells, rigorous polynomial/rational inequalities,
   Bernstein enclosures, interval evaluation with adaptive subdivision, or
   analytic derivative bounds can close sampling gaps. For log/dilog kernels
   use validated elementary-function evaluation. Prove thresholds/UV tails
   separately rather than asking ordinary Gauss quadrature to discover them.
5. Give a rounded OUTWARD bound after adding all errors/tails. Report a finite
   witness's bound, rather than an extrapolated optimum, as the certificate.
6. A first target can be a slightly weaker compact-U max-g0 certificate. Add
   odd source nulls and more crossing derivatives only after that pipeline is
   reliable; they can then strengthen it without destroying compact U.
